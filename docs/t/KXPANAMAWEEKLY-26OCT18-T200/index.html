Will there be more than 200 transit calls through the Panama Canal from Oct 12, 2026 to Oct 18, 2026? Above 200

Pass (Claude: 22% chance of YES) Settled NO

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

IMF PortWatch counted about 27 Panama Canal transits a day (about 189 a week) in the week ending Oct 4, 2026, while the canal authority capped traffic at 32 slots a day because of drought. Capacity rises to 33 a day only from Oct 15, which at PortWatch's usual ratio of about 85% of the cap adds only a few transits, for an expected total of about 192-196. Getting above 200 needs a week roughly 4-5% better than that, which is possible given normal week-to-week swings but less likely than not.

Wrong if: PortWatch's 7-day average rising to about 29 or more a day by mid-October (for example, an unexpectedly fast recovery after the Oct 15 capacity increase), giving a weekly total of 201 or more.

After seeing the price

The market's ~0.195 price likely reflects newer PortWatch data that I couldn't see, so I lowered my estimate from 0.27 to 0.22. The remaining gap to the 0.20 ask is too small to justify a bet.

How the market resolves

If the number of transit calls through the Panama Canal as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 200 from Oct 12, 2026 to Oct 18, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The total is calculated by summing daily counts from Oct 12, 2026 to Oct 18, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
