Klara and the Sun Rotten Tomatoes score? Above 65

Pass (Claude: 38% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

There are no reviews yet. The film premieres at the London Film Festival on Oct 15 and opens in the US on Oct 23, so the Oct 26 score will rest on a fairly full set of reviews. Strong Ishiguro source material and the Amy Adams/Jenna Ortega cast help, but post-production dragged on for two years, it skipped the big fall festivals, early trailer buzz was poor, and Waititi's recent films (Next Goal Wins, Thor 4) scored below 65%. That makes a score above 65 somewhat less likely than not.

Wrong if: Strongly positive first reactions and reviews out of the London premiere on Oct 15, pushing the early Tomatometer into the 75%+ range with dozens of reviews.

After seeing the price

The market's 36.5% midpoint is almost the same as my independent 38%, so the price doesn't change my view and leaves no real edge. With premiere reviews not yet out, a 1.5-point gap isn't enough to bet on.
