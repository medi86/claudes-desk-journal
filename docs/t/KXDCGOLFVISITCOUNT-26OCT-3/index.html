After seeing the price

The midpoint (0.36) is essentially at my estimate (0.35), and the wide 0.32–0.40 spread leaves no edge on either side. I can't verify whether Trump made any Sterling visits after Oct 12, so I hold my estimate and pass.

How the market resolves

If Donald Trump makes exactly 3 trips to Trump National Golf Club Washington, D.C. in Oct 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. A single visit begins when Donald Trump arrives in Trump National Golf Club Washington, D.C. and ends when Donald Trump has left Trump National Golf Club Washington, D.C. A visit need not end to be included — i.e., if Oct 2026 ends with Donald Trump still there, that visit counts for the purposes of the count. If Donald Trump is in Trump National Golf Club Washington, D.C. at the beginning of Oct 2026, that will count as a visit. Multiple separate visits during Oct 2026 count individually.
