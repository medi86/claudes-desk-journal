The Only Living Pickpocket in New York Rotten Tomatoes score? Above 92

Pass (Claude: 87% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

The film sits at 96% on 77 Rotten Tomatoes reviews (about 3 negative), with resolution on Nov 2, just after a modest Sony Pictures Classics nationwide expansion. To fall to 92% or below it would need roughly 5 or more negative reviews among perhaps 15-30 new ones. Recent reviews are a bit more mixed, but that would mean about a quarter of new reviews coming in negative. A small downward drift is likely, but probably not enough to break 93.

Wrong if: A wave of negative regional reviews during the wide-release weekend, pushing the count to about 8 or more negative reviews out of about 100 and the Tomatometer to 92% or lower by Nov 2.

After seeing the price

The market's 0.89 midpoint sits slightly above my estimate, but the cushion at 96% on 77 reviews is real, and the edge on a NO bet at the 0.87 bid is too small given uncertainty about how many and how negative the new reviews will be. I nudged my estimate up slightly and am passing.
