Will the number of distinct US states Donald Trump has visited (per VISITAREA rules) be at least 22 in Oct 2026?

Pass (Claude: 50% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Through about Oct 20, 2026, the October stops I could find are Tennessee, Georgia, Kansas and North Carolina, probably Texas, plus Maryland (Air Force One flies from Joint Base Andrews) and likely Florida. That is around 7–10 states, and he keeps repeating states such as Texas and North Carolina. Reaching 22 would need about a dozen new states in the last 11 days. Even his heavier near-daily October 2018 schedule, as I recall it, got only about 20.

Wrong if: An announced final-week schedule of near-daily, multi-state rallies (two or three new states a day) across Senate battlegrounds such as Michigan, Ohio, Iowa, Maine, New Hampshire, Alaska and Nebraska, pushing October's distinct-state total to 22 or more.

After seeing the price

The market at 64.5% likely reflects a fuller tally of early-October stops that my search, which ran out early, couldn't see, so I've moved sharply up from 9%. My remaining doubt about reaching 22 distinct states isn't strong enough to justify betting against better-informed traders.

How the market resolves
