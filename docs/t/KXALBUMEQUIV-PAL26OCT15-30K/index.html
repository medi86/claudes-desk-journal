Will Palace For The People by Greta Van Fleet have above 30000 Album Equivalent Units during the October 09, 2026 - October 15, 2026 tracking week? Above 30K

Pass (Claude: 65% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Greta Van Fleet's last album, Starcatcher, debuted with 45K units (41K actual sales). If I remember right, the one before had about 41K, which suggests a steady physical-buying fanbase. The new album has many signed and exclusive vinyl versions, but three years have passed since the last album and reviews are lukewarm, so a typical 20–35% drop would leave it around 30–36K. My median is somewhat above the 30K line.

Wrong if: First-week reports (HITS, Billboard or Luminate) putting Palace for the People below 30K units, e.g. a debut outside the top 15 with ~25K units because sales fell more than expected or vinyl shipped late.

After seeing the price

The market sits a little above my estimate and may reflect early first-week projections I couldn't verify, so I nudged up to 0.65. That leaves no meaningful edge against either side of the spread.
