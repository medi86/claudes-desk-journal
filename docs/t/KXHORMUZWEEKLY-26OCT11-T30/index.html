Will there be more than 30 transit calls through the Strait of Hormuz from Oct 5, 2026 to Oct 11, 2026? Above 30

Buy YES · 1 contract · bid 12¢ (from 22%) Filled 1.00 of 1.00 Open Price seen, excluded from blind score

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

PortWatch has recently been recording about 2–4 Hormuz transits a day, which works out to roughly 21–28 a week, under the 30 threshold. Other AIS trackers show a modest pickup on Oct 5–7. Scaling their counts by the recent PortWatch ratio gives an estimate of about 25–30 for the week. Escalating Iranian attacks and threats to close more routes make a higher count less likely.

Wrong if: PortWatch publishes daily counts for Oct 5-11 averaging 4.5 or more a day (e.g., 6+ on Oct 5 and Oct 7), pushing the weekly total to 31 or more.

After seeing the price

PortWatch has published nothing for October 5–11 yet, so traders lack official counts too. Other trackers show traffic up on October 5–7, and scaling those counts by the recent PortWatch ratio puts the week near 25–30, so landing above 30 is quite possible. I moved down from 35% toward the market given how thin that ratio is, but 22% is still above the 12.5% midpoint.

How the market resolves

If the number of transit calls through the Strait of Hormuz as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 30 from Oct 5, 2026 to Oct 11, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The total is calculated by summing daily counts from Oct 5, 2026 to Oct 11, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
