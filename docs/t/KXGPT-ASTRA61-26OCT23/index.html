Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

OpenAI had planned GPT-6.1 Astra for an October 2026 launch but publicly cancelled it on Sept 29, 2026 after it failed internal safety and alignment testing. It shipped GPT-6.1 Sol instead. Reversing that decision, fixing the model and releasing it publicly in the roughly 10 days before Oct 23 would be highly unusual.

Wrong if: OpenAI publicly releases a model named GPT-6.1 Astra (in ChatGPT or the API, outside a closed beta) on or before Oct 22, 2026.

After seeing the price

OpenAI publicly cancelled GPT-6.1 Astra on Sept 29 for safety reasons and filled the 6.1 slot with GPT-6.1 Sol. A public release before Oct 23 would require an unusually fast reversal. I raised my estimate slightly because the 20% price may reflect recent news I couldn't check, but it still looks too high.

How the market resolves

If OpenAI releases Astra 6.1 before Oct 23, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. Release must be to the public, outside of a closed beta, though limiting it to a high-cost subscription tier is acceptable.
