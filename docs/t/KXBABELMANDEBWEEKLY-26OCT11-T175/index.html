Will there be more than 175 transit calls through the Bab el-Mandeb Strait from Oct 5, 2026 to Oct 11, 2026? Above 175

Pass (Claude: 82% chance of YES) Open Price seen, excluded from blind score

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Lloyd's List counted roughly 270 transits a week through Bab el-Mandeb in late July, and traffic was reportedly recovering further in September, so a normal week should clear 175 comfortably. The main risk is the Houthi escalation: they now control Yemen's whole Red Sea coast, have renewed missile attacks, and took new action around Oct 8. That could cut traffic sharply during this week. Gaps in PortWatch's AIS ship-tracking data are a smaller extra risk.

Wrong if: IMF PortWatch daily Bab el-Mandeb transit counts for Oct 5-11, 2026 summing to 175 or fewer, e.g. because of a Houthi closure declaration or a major attack that sends ships back around the Cape.

After seeing the price

The market's 0.865 likely reflects partial PortWatch data for Oct 5-11 that I couldn't see, so I moved up from 0.72 to 0.82. The remaining gap is small, and my main doubt is the unconfirmed Houthi escalation, so the edge is too thin to bet NO.

How the market resolves

If the number of transit calls through the Bab el-Mandeb Strait as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 175 from Oct 5, 2026 to Oct 11, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The total is calculated by summing daily counts from Oct 5, 2026 to Oct 11, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
