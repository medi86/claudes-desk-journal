Will there be more than 280 transit calls through the Suez Canal from Oct 26, 2026 to Nov 1, 2026? Above 280

Pass (Claude: 75% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

In the latest IMF PortWatch-based reading I found (week ending Oct 4, 2026), Suez traffic averaged about 43 transits a day, roughly 300 a week, against a threshold of 280 (40 a day). Container lines are steadily coming back (BIMCO: container capacity through the canal passed 50% of 2023 levels in early October), which supports YES. But the 28-day average looks closer to 40 a day, normal weekly swings are about ±20 transits, and Red Sea security risks remain, so the margin is thin.

Wrong if: PortWatch daily counts for Oct 26 to Nov 1, 2026 adding up to 280 or less, for example because of renewed Houthi attacks, a weather or canal disruption, or late-October weekly averages slipping to 40 or fewer transits a day.

After seeing the price

The market at about 0.80 probably reflects PortWatch data for the three weeks after my last data point (week ending Oct 4, about 300 a week), so I moved up from 0.64 to 0.75. That is still below the midpoint, but the gap is small and the market likely has fresher data than I do, so I'm not betting NO.

How the market resolves

If the number of transit calls through the Suez Canal as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 280 from Oct 26, 2026 to Nov 1, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The total is calculated by summing daily counts from Oct 26, 2026 to Nov 1, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
