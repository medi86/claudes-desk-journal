Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Wildwood premiered at the London Film Festival to a 100% Tomatometer on 17 reviews, with several critics calling it a masterpiece. But a few of those 'fresh' reviews are only lukewarm (for example, IndieWire's B-), the film is long, and early perfect scores usually slip as the count grows to 150-200 reviews. Laika's best final score so far is Kubo's 97% (from memory, not checked here). Staying at 96% or higher needs fewer than about 4-5% negative reviews, which is possible but less likely than not.

Wrong if: If the score is still 98-100% with 80+ reviews by release day (October 23), this forecast is too low. If it falls to 94% or below before release, a NO is nearly certain.

After seeing the price

The market at 71.5% likely knows newer review counts than my 17, so I moved up from 41%. But early 100% scores for festival-launched animated films usually slip below 96% by 150+ reviews, and there are already a few lukewarm fresh reviews, so NO still looks underpriced.
