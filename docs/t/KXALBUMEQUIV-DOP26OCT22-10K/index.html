Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Dopamine Chamber's release on Oct 16, 2026 makes the Oct 16-22 window its first week. The previous album, Romance, debuted at No. 97 on the Billboard 200 in 2024, which I estimate was roughly 8-10K US units, so it was near this threshold. The band has grown since then (UK first-week sales doubled, Grammy nominations, a Rosalía feature, vinyl first-pressing pre-orders), so a debut above 10K seems more likely than not. It is far from certain because the US audience is still modest.

Wrong if: Luminate reports Dopamine Chamber at 10,000 or fewer US album-equivalent units for the Oct 16-22, 2026 week, for example a debut in the lower half of the Billboard 200 or below it.
