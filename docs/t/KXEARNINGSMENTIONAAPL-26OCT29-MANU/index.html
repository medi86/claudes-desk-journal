What will Apple say during their next earnings call? Manufacturing

Buy YES · 1 contract · bid 88¢ (from 93%) Resting Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Apple's next earnings call is November 2, 2026, before the market closes. Apple executives said 'manufacturing' several times in each recent call, mostly about the American Manufacturing Program, the Broadcom deal and the upcoming Houston Advanced Manufacturing Center. An update on that center and the U.S. investment program is very likely, even with a new CEO. The word also comes up often in Q&A about tariffs and supply chains.

Wrong if: John Ternus's first call on Nov 2 leaves out the U.S. investment/American Manufacturing Program section, and no Apple representative says 'manufacturing' in the Q&A (or the call is pushed past Nov 12).

After seeing the price

The market mid of 0.865 likely reflects the risk that the new CEO changes the script, so I've shaded my estimate down slightly. Repeated use of 'American Manufacturing Program', the pending update on the Houston center, and tariff/supply Q&A still put my probability above the 0.89 ask.

How the market resolves
