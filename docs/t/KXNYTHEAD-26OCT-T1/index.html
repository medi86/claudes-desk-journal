Will the number of full-width New York Times front page headlines be at least 1 in Oct 2026?

Pass (Claude: 22% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

The NYT runs full-width banner headlines only for major news, roughly a few times a year, so any given month has maybe a 20-35% chance. October 2026 has no scheduled mega-event because the midterm results will land in November, and about two-thirds of the month seems to have passed without a banner I could find, though my search was very limited. That leaves a surprise in the remaining ~10 days or an early-October banner I missed as the main ways to YES.

Wrong if: An NYT Store front-page reprint for any October 2026 date showing a standalone, centered, full-width banner headline, e.g., for a sudden war escalation, the death of a major world figure, or a large disaster.

After seeing the price

The midpoint (0.255) is only slightly above my estimate, and the wide spread means buying NO at about 0.79 has essentially no edge. The market may also reflect early-October events I couldn't check, so I nudge my estimate toward it and pass.

How the market resolves

If the number of New York Times front page banner headlines is at least 1 in Oct 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. How this is resolved This market resolves based on the front page displayed in the NYT Store's 'Front Page Reprint' section. "Banner headline" refers to a standalone centered headline surrounded only by whitespace. Past examples: Jan 5, 2026; Jan 4, 2026; Nov 5, 2025; May 31, 2024; Jun 24, 2016
