Sense and Sensibility Rotten Tomatoes score? Above 80

Pass (Claude: 33% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

With 39 reviews, mostly from UK critics, the film sits at 79% on Rotten Tomatoes, and it has stayed between 75% and 82% since its debut. A 7.0/10 average and a Metacritic score of 72 point to a final score right around 80. To resolve YES, the page needs to show at least 81%, so the incoming US reviews would have to be noticeably more positive than the UK ones. That could happen, but it is less likely than not.

Wrong if: The US release on Oct 16 brings a wave of mostly positive American reviews that lifts the displayed Tomatometer to 81% or higher by the morning of Oct 19.

After seeing the price

The market's 0.34 midpoint is almost exactly my independent estimate of 0.33, so the price doesn't give me a reason to change my view. The gap is too small to justify a bet either way.
