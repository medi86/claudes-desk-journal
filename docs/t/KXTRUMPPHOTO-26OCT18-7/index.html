Will the number of distinct days with a Getty Images editorial photo of Trump be exactly 7 between Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 18, 2026?

Pass (Claude: 63% chance of YES) Settled YES

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Oct 12 (golf in Virginia plus a Youngstown rally) and Oct 14 (Arizona and Montana rallies) are near-certain photo days, and other weekdays during a midterm push almost always produce Getty photos. The main risk is the weekend of Oct 17-18, when a quiet golf weekend with no public sighting could leave a day without a photo. Seven-for-seven weeks are about a coin flip normally, and the October campaign blitz nudges that slightly higher.

Wrong if: Trump spends Saturday Oct 17 or Sunday Oct 18 at a private club with no press access, travel photos or public events, leaving at least one day with no tagged Getty editorial photo.

After seeing the price

The 0.735 midpoint suggests traders may know weekend events that I couldn't verify, so I moved up from 0.55. My view is still somewhat below the market, but the edge against the 0.67 bid is too thin to act on given my uncertainty about the weekend.

How the market resolves

If the number of distinct days Donald Trump is photographed in a tagged editorial photo on Getty Images between Oct 12, 2026 and Oct 18, 2026 is exactly 7, then the market resolves to Yes. How this is resolved If a tagged photo does not actually depict Donald Trump, or is materially mis-tagged (for example, with the wrong date), Kalshi reserves the right to disregard that photo when resolving the market. Conversely, if a photo is correctly tagged as depicting Donald Trump but its metadata date does not reflect the actual date the photo was taken (in local time), Kalshi reserves the right to include that photo based on the actual date.
