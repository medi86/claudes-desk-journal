Will at least 125,000,000 votes, including blank and null votes, be cast in the runoff of Brazil's 2026 presidential election?

Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 56¢ (from 41% YES) Not placed: rejected by Kalshi (invalid_order) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

First-round 2026 turnout was about 125.28M, only about 280k above the 125M threshold, so the runoff needs abstention to rise no more than about 0.18 points from 21.08%. In 5 of the last 6 presidential runoffs, abstention rose by 1 to 3 points. The exception, 2022 (abstention fell 0.36 points), was a close, polarized Lula-vs-Bolsonaro race much like this one. Record first-round disaffection and two widely rejected candidates make a small turnout drop slightly more likely than a gain.

Wrong if: TSE-certified runoff turnout of 125.0M or more (abstention at or below about 21.26%), e.g., a 2022-style mobilization surge in a knife-edge Lula vs Flávio race.

After seeing the price

The 0.44 midpoint sits slightly above my estimate. The historical tendency for runoff abstention to rise, plus record first-round disaffection, makes clearing a threshold only about 280,000 votes below first-round turnout somewhat less likely than the market implies. The edge is small, but it favors NO.

How the market resolves

If at least 125,000,000 votes, including blank and null votes, are cast in the second round of the 2026 Brazilian presidential election, then the market resolves to Yes. The count is the total of voters who voted (comparecimento) in the presidential contest of the second round (runoff) of Brazil’s 2026 general election, as reported by the Tribunal Superior Eleitoral (TSE), including those who cast blank or null votes. Registered voters, abstentions, justified absences and ballots in other contests do not count. Subject to the contract’s Fully-Conducted Elections and Accelerated Resolution Upon Designated Media Source Declarations provisions, the vote count refers to the officially certified results from the relevant election authority. Provisional, absentee, early, mail-in, or electronic ballots are included only if reflected in the official certification. The contract resolves based on officially certified results regardless of ongoing appeals. Outside those two contract provisions, preliminary counts, exit polls, projections, or uncertified results do not qualify.
