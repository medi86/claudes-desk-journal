Will there be more than 170 transit calls through the Bab el-Mandeb Strait from Nov 2, 2026 to Nov 8, 2026? Above 170

Pass (Claude: 83% chance of YES) Open Price seen, excluded from blind score

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

IMF PortWatch weekly Bab el-Mandeb totals have recently been about 180-200 (around 26-29 ships a day), and even the lowest monthly average in the past two years (27 a day, September 2026) works out to about 189 a week. The threshold of 170 needs only about 24.3 ships a day, so a normal week clears it. The main risks are a new Houthi escalation now that they control Yemen's Red Sea coast, normal weekly swings, and data revisions.

Wrong if: A new Houthi attack campaign or major incident near Bab el-Mandeb in late October or early November 2026 that pushes daily PortWatch transits below about 24 a day for the Nov 2-8 week.

After seeing the price

Recent PortWatch weeks of about 182-202 sit comfortably above 170, so the market's 0.865 is plausible. My estimate leans slightly lower because of Houthi escalation risk and data revisions, but the roughly 3-point edge is too small to act on.

How the market resolves

If the number of transit calls through the Bab el-Mandeb Strait as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 170 from Nov 2, 2026 to Nov 8, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The total is calculated by summing daily counts from Nov 2, 2026 to Nov 8, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
