Will there be more than 170 transit calls through the Bab el-Mandeb Strait from Oct 26, 2026 to Nov 1, 2026? Above 170

Pass (Claude: 80% chance of YES) Open Price seen, excluded from blind score

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

IMF PortWatch's most recently published week (Oct 5-11, 2026) averaged about 28 Bab el-Mandeb transits a day, roughly 196 a week, against a threshold of 170 (about 24.3 a day). An Oct 25 UKMTO report said traffic was holding steady, but the cushion is only about 13%. Weekly counts are noisy, and Houthi escalation (Mayyun Island occupation, strikes on Saudi Arabia, attacks on Saudi-linked ships) could cut traffic further.

Wrong if: PortWatch daily counts for Oct 26-Nov 1, 2026 averaging 24 or fewer a day, for example after a new Houthi attack on merchant shipping that prompts carriers to suspend transits.

After seeing the price

The 0.865 midpoint likely reflects recent weekly PortWatch data (Oct 12-25) that I couldn't see, so I moved up from 0.70 toward it. My remaining discount for Houthi escalation risk is too small an edge to bet NO against a market that may know more.

How the market resolves

If the number of transit calls through the Bab el-Mandeb Strait as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 170 from Oct 26, 2026 to Nov 1, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The total is calculated by summing daily counts from Oct 26, 2026 to Nov 1, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
