Will Morgan Wallen have above 7.5M daily views at any point during October 2026?

Pass (Claude: 12% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Kworb shows Morgan Wallen's official-channel videos averaging about 2.6M views a day. Adding audio-only Topic tracks and fan uploads likely puts his YouTube Charts global total around 6.5–8M a day, close to the 7.5M line. The market needs only one day above 7.5M in October, which helps, but the tour has ended, the album is more than a year old and I found no confirmed October release, so I lean slightly toward NO. I couldn't check the actual YouTube Charts number, so confidence is low.

Wrong if: YouTube Charts showing Wallen's global daily views already regularly above 7.5M, or a new single or album dropping in October 2026 that pushes a single day above 7.5M.

After seeing the price

The low price late in October suggests the 7.5M mark hasn't been hit yet, with only about nine days left. My 40% rested on a guessed multiplier, so I'm deferring to the market. With a wide spread and no clear edge, I'm passing.
