Will SpaceX launch another Starship before Nov 13, 2026?

Pass (Claude: 18% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Flight 15 hardware has completed static fires, but the latest reported target (an FAA slide via SpaceNotices/Starlust, Oct 29-30) is no earlier than Nov 12, 2026, which is the final eligible day under the resolution rules. Starship launches usually slip by days because of scrubs, weather, or approvals (Flights 13 and 14 both did), and this flight attempts a first-ever ship catch that likely needs extra FAA sign-off. A YES therefore needs a launch on the NET date or earlier with no slip at all.

Wrong if: Starship Flight 15 lifting off from Starbase on or before November 12, 2026 (local time), e.g., if FAA approval arrives quickly and SpaceX pulls the date to ~Nov 8 or launches on the first Nov 12 attempt.

After seeing the price

The market's 15.5% midpoint sits slightly below my estimate but agrees that a NET Nov 12 target leaves almost no slack. The market may also reflect fresher schedule or approval news I couldn't verify, so I've shaded to 18%. The remaining edge is too small to bet.

How the market resolves

If SpaceX launches Starship flight test number 15 After issuance and before Nov 13, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. For the purpose of this market, the launch date is the calendar date on which liftoff occurred, measured in the local timezone of the launch site, as publicly confirmed by SpaceX
