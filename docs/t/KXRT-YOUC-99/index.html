You Can See Everything Rotten Tomatoes score? Above 99

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

The film currently sits at 100% on Rotten Tomatoes from about 22 reviews, with a 98 Metacritic score and no mixed or negative reviews. But resolution requires it to stay perfect through release week, when the review count will likely grow to around 100 or more. A long, ethically divisive Elizabeth Holmes documentary is likely to draw at least one negative review, and most festival darlings that open at 100% end up at 95-99%.

Wrong if: The film still shows 100% with 100+ reviews on Oct 19, 2026 (or, conversely, any single rotten review appearing before then confirms the NO lean).

After seeing the price

The market at 23.5% is close to my estimate; traders likely see a more current review count than my 22-review snapshot, so I moved toward them. What's left is a gap of under three points, too small to act on given how uncertain the forecast is.
