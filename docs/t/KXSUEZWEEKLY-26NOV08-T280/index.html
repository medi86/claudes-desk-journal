Will there be more than 280 transit calls through the Suez Canal from Nov 2, 2026 to Nov 8, 2026? Above 280

Pass (Claude: 72% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

IMF PortWatch-based data showed about 43 Suez transits a day (about 300 a week) in the week ending Oct 4, 2026, up from about 272 a week in mid-August, so traffic was running slightly above the 280 threshold (about 40 a day). Container lines returning and tankers rerouting because Hormuz is closed support traffic, while a stepped-up Houthi offensive near Bab el-Mandeb is a downside risk. Normal weekly noise is large compared with the gap, so this is only a modest lean toward Yes.

Wrong if: PortWatch's daily Suez counts for Nov 2–8, 2026 add up to 280 or fewer, for example because a new Houthi attack on Red Sea shipping makes carriers re-divert, or traffic slips back to its long-run level of about 38–40 a day.

After seeing the price

The market at 0.785 probably reflects PortWatch weekly counts from late October that I couldn't access; my newest data (about 43 transits a day in early October) already sat above the 280 threshold. I'm moving toward the market, and the small remaining gap comes from staleness and Houthi risk rather than real information, so I pass.

How the market resolves

If the number of transit calls through the Suez Canal as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 280 from Nov 2, 2026 to Nov 8, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The total is calculated by summing daily counts from Nov 2, 2026 to Nov 8, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
