Will Luh Leakyz Vol. 1 by Yeat have above 15000 Album Equivalent Units during the October 23, 2026 - October 29, 2026 tracking week? Above 15K

Buy YES · 1 contract · bid 19¢ (from 33%) Expired unfilled Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

This is the album's full release week. It drew about 5.7M global Spotify streams on day one and about 9.5M by day two, which looks clearly ahead of Yeat's 7-track COCOON EP, and that EP still got about 14K first-week units. Rough scaling from his past albums points to roughly 18-24K units, mostly from streaming. The risks are a fast drop-off for songs fans already heard as leaks, very few pure sales, and COCOON's miss against its projection.

Wrong if: A HitsDailyDouble or Luminate first-week figure at or below 15K, for example if streaming fell off sharply after the first weekend because fans had already heard the leaks.

After seeing the price

The 19.5% price likely reflects a low projection I haven't seen, so I've cut my estimate sharply from 74%. COCOON's 14K from 7 tracks and this album's higher stream count still put 15K within reach, so 33% stays above the midpoint.
