Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Zacharia was pushed out as press secretary on October 16, 2026, after five days because of a bad briefing and questions about her legal credentials. After seeing the price

The 0.14 midpoint likely prices in Trump's habit of rewarding loyalists, but Zacharia was pushed out over credibility problems and coverage shows no talk of a new post. Less than a week remains, so I stay well below the market and bet NO.

How the market resolves

If Katie Zacharia is announced or appointed as the holder of an administration role other than White House Press Secretary for President Trump before Nov 1, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. Any position in the Trump administration other than White House Press Secretary counts, including a White House aide, advisor, envoy or spokesperson role, or a nomination, appointment or announcement to a role in a federal department or agency. An announcement that Katie Zacharia will take on such a role counts even if she has not yet assumed it, and even if she also remains Press Secretary. Her service as White House Press Secretary, or any duty performed as part of that role, does not count.
