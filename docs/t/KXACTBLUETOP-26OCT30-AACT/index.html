Will Amy Acton be the top ActBlue fundraiser on Oct 30 at 10:00 AM ET?

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

About four days before this forecast, Amy Acton was ActBlue's second-highest fundraiser of the week, after a backlash against an attack ad. A week ending Oct 30 will mostly miss that spike. In the last week before the election, Senate candidates with national donor networks (for example Talarico) usually lead the list. She could only overtake them with a new viral surge, so YES is unlikely.

Wrong if: A new viral moment or controversy in Ohio in the final days pushes Acton's small-dollar fundraising above every Senate candidate's, putting her in the leftmost spot on ActBlue's weekly list on Oct 30.

After seeing the price

The 16.5% price is somewhat above my estimate. I shaded up to 12% because my research stopped before confirming the current leader. A week ending Oct 30 mostly misses the spike after the ad, and Senate candidates usually lead the final pre-election week, so I modestly favor NO.
