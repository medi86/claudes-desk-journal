Will Trump say "Golden Dome" before Nov 1, 2026?

Buy YES · 1 contract · bid 37¢ (from 78%) Resting Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Golden Dome is one of Trump's signature projects. He has repeatedly named it in speeches and on Truth Social, and it is in the news this month because of a funding fight. He is giving midterm rally speeches almost daily through October, so a direct quote between Oct 1 and Nov 1 is very likely. I couldn't confirm a specific October quote, so some risk remains that he sticks to domestic campaign themes.

Wrong if: A transcript review of Trump's October 1-31, 2026 rallies, interviews, and Truth Social posts finds no instance of him saying or writing 'Golden Dome'.

After seeing the price

The low price hints that traders may have checked and found no October mention so far, so I lowered my estimate. With frequent rallies and an active Golden Dome funding story, I still think a mention before Nov 1 is well above the ask.

How the market resolves

If Golden Dome, or a plural or possessive form of Golden Dome, is stated by Donald Trump after October 1, 2026 at 10:00am ET and before Nov 1, 2026 at 12:00am ET, then the market resolves to Yes.
