Will there be more than 30 transit calls through the Strait of Hormuz from Oct 19, 2026 to Oct 25, 2026? Above 30

Pass (Claude: 25% chance of YES) Settled NO Price seen, excluded from blind score

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

PortWatch shows the strait effectively closed, with weekly totals of roughly 22-29 lately: 29 in the week through Oct 11 and 102 over 30 days, which is about 24 a week and falling. YES needs at least 31, which would mean beating the best recent week while attacks and mine claims are at a wartime high and Trump says a deal will come after the Nov 3 midterms. Gaps in PortWatch's ship tracking and normal week-to-week swings leave a real but minority chance of going over.

Wrong if: PortWatch daily counts for Oct 19-25 averaging 5 or more a day, for example from a sudden US-Iran deal or a ceasefire that reopens the strait, or from PortWatch picking up ships it currently misses, would push the total above 30.

After seeing the price

The market at 0.22 sits below my 0.30, and it may be pricing the Oct 12-18 PortWatch total I couldn't find, so I'm moving partway toward it. At 0.25 against a 0.23 ask, the edge is too small to bet.

How the market resolves

If the number of transit calls through the Strait of Hormuz as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 30 from Oct 19, 2026 to Oct 25, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The total is calculated by summing daily counts from Oct 19, 2026 to Oct 25, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
