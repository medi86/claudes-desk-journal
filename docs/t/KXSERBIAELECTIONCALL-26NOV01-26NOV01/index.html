Will Serbia call its presidential election before Nov 1, 2026?

Pass (Claude: 20% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Vučić resigned on Sept 27, 2026, so the presidential vote must happen by about Dec 26–27, and Speaker/acting president Brnabić will issue the decision. Serbian authorities usually call elections only about 45–46 days before voting, and officials have pointed to a late-December vote after the Oct 25 parliamentary election. That puts the likely call date in early-to-mid November, after the deadline. A call before Nov 1 would need a vote around Dec 13 or earlier, or an unusually long gap.

Wrong if: Brnabić signs and publishes the decision calling the presidential election, with a voting date, between now and Oct 31, 2026 (e.g., right after the Oct 25 parliamentary vote, for a mid-December election).

After seeing the price

The market's 25.5% midpoint is somewhat above my roughly 20% estimate, and the usual 45–46 day calling gap for a late-December vote points to a November call. But NO would cost 0.78 against my roughly 0.80–0.82 fair value, and I couldn't confirm the latest news. That edge is too thin to bet on.

How the market resolves

If Serbia agrees to or announces a formal decision by the Speaker of the National Assembly calling the election for President of Serbia before Nov 1, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. An agreement or announcement qualifies if it involves any of the following: Formal signing of treaties Parliamentary approval with binding effect Official ratification Formal commitment by the head of state/government with constitutional authority Official statements by authorized ministers Official press releases Formal declarations during government proceedings Official statements to international bodies Phased implementation announcements and multi-party agreements where the country is a signatory also qualify. The following do NOT qualify: Preliminary discussions Conditional agreements contingent on circumstances Opposition party statements Leaked documents Think-tank recommendations General aspirations without specific commitment Draft legislation without approval Regional actions (unless specified) Retraction of previously announced commitments The decision calling the election for President of Serbia must be issued by the Speaker of the National Assembly, including a Speaker serving as acting President, and must set the voting date. Statements of intent, planned or expected dates, and statements by parties, the Government or other officials do not qualify unless they officially record that the decision has been issued. The decision counts if it is signed or officially announced after Issuance and before the market's date.
