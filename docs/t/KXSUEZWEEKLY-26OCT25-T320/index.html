Will there be more than 320 transit calls through the Suez Canal from Oct 19, 2026 to Oct 25, 2026? Above 320

Pass (Claude: 25% chance of YES) Settled NO

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

The latest PortWatch week (ending Oct 4) averaged about 43 transits a day, or about 301 for the week. The market needs more than 320, about 45.7 a day. Container lines are returning quickly (Maersk and Hapag-Lloyd moved services in late September, ONE and HMM are expected next, and container capacity is back above 50% of pre-crisis levels), and single days have already hit 46. But clearing the line needs about 7% growth in three weeks, so it is close to a coin flip with a slight lean to NO.

Wrong if: PortWatch data for the weeks ending Oct 11 and Oct 18 showing averages of 46 a day or more (would favor YES), or a new Houthi attack on a merchant ship in the Red Sea in mid-October that halts the carrier return (would favor NO).

After seeing the price

The 18.5% market price probably reflects the PortWatch week ending Oct 11, which I could not see and which likely came in well below 320, so I cut my estimate from 0.44 to 0.25. The container return still argues for some upside, but the remaining gap is too uncertain to bet on.

How the market resolves

If the number of transit calls through the Suez Canal as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 320 from Oct 19, 2026 to Oct 25, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The total is calculated by summing daily counts from Oct 19, 2026 to Oct 25, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
