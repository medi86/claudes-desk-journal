Will Trump make 3 trips to Mar-a-Lago as President in Oct 2026?

Pass (Claude: 38% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Trump's first Mar-a-Lago trip of the season began Oct 16, 2026. His Kentucky detour on Oct 17 ended back in Palm Beach, so it counts as the same visit. A second trip around the Oct 23 southwest Florida rally looks likely, so exactly three would need another separate trip, most plausibly the Oct 30-31 Halloween weekend, as in 2025. But the last week of midterm campaigning and the possibility of chaining stops without returning to the White House make two trips about as likely as three.

Wrong if: Trump does not return to Mar-a-Lago after an Oct 23 weekend visit, or goes from Florida straight to campaign stops without returning to the White House, leaving the October count at 2 or fewer; or he makes a fourth separate trip.

After seeing the price

The market's 0.405 midpoint is close to my estimate, and it may reflect schedule details I couldn't research. A gap of about 2.5 points isn't enough of an edge to bet on, given my uncertainty about whether trips get merged and the late-October campaign schedule.

How the market resolves

If the number of trips Trump makes to Mar-a-Lago in Oct 2026 is exactly 3, then the market resolves to Yes. How this is resolved A single visit begins when the President arrives in Mar-a-Lago and ends when the President has returned to the White House. A visit need not end to be included - i.e. if Oct 2026 ends with the President still there, that visit counts for the purposes of the count. If the President is in Mar-a-Lago at the beginning of Oct 2026, that will count as a visit.
