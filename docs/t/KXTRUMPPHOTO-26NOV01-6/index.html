Will the number of distinct days with a Getty Images editorial photo of Trump be exactly 6 between Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 1, 2026?

Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 82¢ (from 12% YES) Filled 1.00 of 1.00 Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Trump has a public rally scheduled or already held on every day from Oct 26 to Nov 1 (MS, TX, OH, SC, FL, KS, IA) ahead of the Nov 3 midterms, and Getty reliably covers these. The most likely count is therefore 7. Exactly 6 needs one cancelled appearance or one day with no Getty-tagged photo, which is fairly unlikely.

Wrong if: One of the Oct 30 Daytona, Oct 31 Salina, or Nov 1 Dubuque rallies is cancelled and Trump has no other photographed appearance that day, or Getty has no tagged photo of Trump for one of the earlier days.

After seeing the price

The market at 17.5% moved me up slightly from 10%, but all seven days have scheduled or completed rallies that Getty normally covers. Seven days looks most likely, and exactly six needs a cancellation or a coverage gap.

How the market resolves

If the number of distinct days Donald Trump is photographed in a tagged editorial photo on Getty Images between Oct 26, 2026 and Nov 1, 2026 is exactly 6, then the market resolves to Yes. How this is resolved If a tagged photo does not actually depict Donald Trump, or is materially mis-tagged (for example, with the wrong date), Kalshi reserves the right to disregard that photo when resolving the market. Conversely, if a photo is correctly tagged as depicting Donald Trump but its metadata date does not reflect the actual date the photo was taken (in local time), Kalshi reserves the right to include that photo based on the actual date.
