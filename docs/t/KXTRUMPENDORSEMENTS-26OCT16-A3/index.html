Will Trump endorse at least 3 people on Truth Social this week?

Buy YES · 1 contract · bid 93¢ (from 96%) Resting Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

The week of Oct 10-16, 2026 is about three weeks before the midterms, when Trump usually posts many 'Complete and Total Endorsement' messages on Truth Social. He was doing so as recently as Oct 7 (Tom Tiffany for Wisconsin governor), and in September he endorsed about a dozen governor candidates. Repeat endorsements count and each person in a multi-candidate post counts, so 3 people in 7 days is a low bar.

Wrong if: Trump makes fewer than 3 clear endorsements on Truth Social between Oct 10 and Oct 16, 2026, for example because he stops posting due to illness or a crisis, or posts only graphics that don't count.

After seeing the price

The market sits around 0.895, but three weeks before the midterms Trump's Truth Social endorsements make 3 qualifying people in a week near-certain. I trimmed my estimate slightly for resolution risk, yet 0.96 is still above the 0.94 ask.

How the market resolves

If Donald Trump endorses at least 3 people for their election on Truth Social from Oct 10, 2026 through Oct 16, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. Any formal endorsement will qualify, including reaffirmations of prior endorsements. If Trump endorses multiple people in an election, all will be counted towards the total. The endorsement must be a clear, affirmative statement of support. Ambiguous statements of support will not qualify. Conditional endorsements will qualify. A person cannot be counted more than once in a given week. Examples: Donald Trump states "I endorse [candidate] for [office]" Donald Trump states "I support [candidate]" referencing a political race Donald Trump states "Vote for [candidate]" publicly Donald Trump states "[candidate] has my full support" for [race] Examples that would NOT count: Trump posts a graphic that says "[Candidate] wins" that merely states as fact they were endorsed by President Trump
