Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

That is roughly 0.23 signing days per week, which gives about a 20% chance of at least one in a given 7-day window. Emergency orders, which NYC mayors issue far more often, don't count.

Wrong if: A Mayor's Office release showing a new numbered (non-emergency) executive order dated between Oct 11 and Oct 17, 2026, e.g., one tied to a policy rollout that week.

After seeing the price

The market's 15.5% sits close to my 20% estimate, which comes from Mamdani signing non-emergency orders on roughly 0.23 days per week since February. My research didn't cover whether anything has been signed since mid-September or is scheduled for that week, so I move toward the market. At 18% versus a 16-cent ask, the edge is too thin to act on.
