Will She’s The Best by Troye Sivan have above 30000 Album Equivalent Units during the October 09, 2026 - October 15, 2026 tracking week? Above 30K

Pass (Claude: 53% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

She's the Best came out Oct 9, 2026, so the Oct 9–15 window is its whole debut week. Sivan's last two albums reportedly debuted in the Billboard 200 top 5 (Bloom with about 38K units, from memory), and his profile has grown since. A total of roughly 18–25K from record sales plus 10–15K from streaming puts it near or slightly above 30K. His modest US streaming and a fast rollout that wasn't built around first-week sales keep this close to a coin flip.

Wrong if: Billboard or Hits Daily Double first-week reporting showing the album debuting with fewer than 30,000 US units (e.g., ~22-28K and a debut outside the top 10).

After seeing the price

The market at 0.48 likely reflects information I couldn't check, such as first-week projections, so I moved my estimate from 0.58 to 0.53. That leaves only a few points above the 0.50 ask, which is too thin an edge for estimates resting partly on unverified memory of prior debuts.
