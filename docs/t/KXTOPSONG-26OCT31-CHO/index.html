Will Choosin' Texas be #1 on the Billboard Hot 100 during the week of Oct 31, 2026

Pass (Claude: 75% chance of YES) Settled YES

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

On the latest chart, Taylor Swift's 'Patient Zero' held No. 1 for a third week and 'Choosin' Texas' was No. 2. Swift's lead comes mostly from very high physical sales (about 134K–151K a week, driven by new CD and vinyl versions) while her streams fell 27%. 'Choosin' Texas' is still the No. 1 streaming song and is barely declining, so it likely returns to No. 1 if Swift's sales drop in the Oct. 16–22 tracking week. Swift's team has kept those sales high with new versions, so I lean slightly against a return.

Wrong if: The Oct. 31 chart shows 'Patient Zero' (or some new debut) at No. 1, for example because another Swift variant keeps physical sales above roughly 80K. Or, the other way, 'Choosin' Texas' takes back No. 1 after Swift's sales collapse.

After seeing the price

A price this high after the tracking week has closed likely reflects early sales projections showing Swift's sales dropped, which I couldn't check, so I moved strongly toward it. My remaining doubt is not enough of an edge to buy NO.
