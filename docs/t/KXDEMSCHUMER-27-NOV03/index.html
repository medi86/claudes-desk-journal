Will any current member of the Democratic Senate caucus (including independents who caucus with Democrats) publicly states that Chuck Schumer should step down, resign, or be replaced as Senate Democratic Leader before Nov 3, 2026?

Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 87¢ (from 10% YES) Resting Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

So far no sitting Senate Democrat has clearly said Schumer should step down. Slotkin's June 2026 comment was hedged and evidently hasn't resolved the market. Only about two weeks remain, the caucus is publicly united behind the midterm push, and senators like Sanders and Warren are deferring the question until after the election. The leadership vote, where any challenge would surface, comes after Nov 3.

Wrong if: A sitting Democratic or caucusing senator (e.g., Slotkin, Murphy, Gallego, Fetterman) explicitly saying before Nov 3 that Schumer should not remain leader or should be replaced, or the market resolver deciding Slotkin's June 2026 remarks count.

After seeing the price

The market's 12-15% sits a bit above my 10%. The likely gap is the chance that Slotkin's ambiguous June remarks get counted, which I already weighted. With only about two weeks left, a united caucus, and the leadership vote after Nov 3, I see a modest edge on NO.

How the market resolves

If any current member of the Democratic Senate caucus (including independents who caucus with Democrats) publicly states that Chuck Schumer should step down, resign, or be replaced as Senate Democratic Leader before Nov 3, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes.
