Will Treg Taylor drop out of the 2026 Alaska Governor race before Nov 3, 2026? Yes

Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 87¢ (from 11% YES) Resting Open Price seen, excluded from blind score

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Taylor made the top-four general election ballot and, about three days ago, publicly refused President Trump's call to drop out and back Bernadette Wilson. He previously fought a disqualification to get back on the ballot. Only about two weeks remain and ballots are already out, so a withdrawal is unlikely. The remaining risk comes from strong pressure to unite Republicans and his last-place polling at around 8-10%.

Wrong if: Taylor announces before Nov 3 that he is suspending or ending his campaign, or endorses Wilson or Bronson while saying he is no longer running.

After seeing the price

The market at 0.14 sits slightly above my 0.11. Taylor's recent public refusal to quit, his earlier fight to stay on the ballot, and the short time left make dropping out unlikely. The edge is modest because Trump's pressure to unite behind Wilson is real.

How the market resolves

If Treg Taylor drops out of the 2026 Alaska Governor race before Nov 3, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. "Dropped out" means any of the following: Treg Taylor announces they are ending, suspending, or withdrawing from their campaign Treg Taylor files official withdrawal paperwork with relevant election authorities Treg Taylor publicly states they are no longer seeking the office Treg Taylor endorses another candidate while stating they are no longer running Treg Taylor is disqualified by election authorities or courts and does not appeal The following do NOT constitute dropping out: Losing a primary or election Failing to qualify for debates or ballots Temporarily suspending campaign activities without withdrawing Being eliminated through the normal election process Reducing campaign activities while remaining a candidate Key provisions: Once someone drops out per above criteria, the market resolves YES even if they later re-enter "Suspending" a campaign is treated as dropping out unless Treg Taylor explicitly states they remain a candidate For primary races, dropping out of the presidential race entirely counts as dropping out of that party's primary Withdrawing from the race counts even if the person remains on the ballot
