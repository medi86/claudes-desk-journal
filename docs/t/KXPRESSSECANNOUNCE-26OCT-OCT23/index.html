Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Katie Zacharia was fired on Oct 16 after five days, so Trump would need to name a permanent replacement within about six days. After Leavitt left, it took him about six weeks to name Zacharia, and the vetting failure argues for a slower search with someone acting in the meantime. Trump's habit of announcing picks impulsively on Truth Social, plus pressure ahead of the midterms, keeps a quick announcement possible.

Wrong if: Trump posts on Truth Social or tells reporters before Oct 23 that a named person (for example Anna Kelly or Steven Cheung) will be the new permanent press secretary.

After seeing the price

My 18% estimate is close to the 19.5% midpoint and sits inside the wide 0.16–0.23 spread, so neither side is worth buying at the actual entry prices. The market's price is consistent with my view that a permanent pick within about six days is unlikely but plausible given Trump's impulsive announcements.
