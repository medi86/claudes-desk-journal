The Mongoose Rotten Tomatoes score? Above 45

Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 71¢ (from 25% YES) Resting Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

The Mongoose is a Samuel Goldwyn-distributed Liam Neeson action thriller directed by Mark Vanselow. Most comparable recent Neeson action films scored below 45% on Rotten Tomatoes (about 3 in 10 cleared it). The trailer drew mockery, and the Rotten Tomatoes page showed 0 reviews just before the October 30 release, suggesting a late embargo. That points to a score at or below 45.

Wrong if: Early critic reviews come in mostly positive, putting the Tomatometer at 50% or higher with 20+ reviews by Nov 2.

After seeing the price

The market's 0.285 is close to my estimate, so I nudged up slightly. The roughly 3-in-10 rate at which comparable Neeson action films clear 45%, plus the 0-review page just before opening and the mocked trailer, still keep me below the market. The edge is modest.
