Will Dai Dai rank #1 on YouTube's Weekly Top Songs Global Chart dated Oct 23 - Oct 29, 2026?

Pass (Claude: 29% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Dai Dai is still #1 on YouTube's global songs chart. In the week ending Oct 22 it had 44.6M views, but its lead over KALYANI (Remix) shrank from about 20% to 6.5% in a single week. Dai Dai is losing roughly 7% of its views per week while KALYANI is growing, and if both trends hold for one more week KALYANI passes it. Noise in the weekly numbers and Dai Dai's long record of holding #1 keep its chances meaningful.

Wrong if: The chart dated Oct 23-29 shows Dai Dai still at #1 because KALYANI (Remix)'s views stalled or fell, or Dai Dai's decline leveled off.

After seeing the price

The lower market price suggests others may see mid-week data favoring KALYANI, so I shaded my 0.34 down to 0.29. With the spread running from 0.18 to 0.31, neither side offers enough edge to trade.
