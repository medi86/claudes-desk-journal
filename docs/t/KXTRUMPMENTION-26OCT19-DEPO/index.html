Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

The Monday October 19 rally for Sen. Roger Marshall was confirmed at a Park City (Wichita-area) arena, with Trump due to speak at 6 p.m. during a nationwide midterm tour. Immigration is a near-constant theme in his rally speeches, and these usually run more than an hour, so saying 'deport', 'deported' or 'deportation' at least once is more likely than not. However, he often uses other phrasings like 'illegal aliens' or 'getting them out', and tense forms like 'deporting' don't count. I couldn't confirm what he actually said.

Wrong if: A full transcript of the Park City/Wichita rally shows Trump discussing immigration only with terms like 'illegal aliens,' 'removed,' or 'deporting' and never saying 'deport,' 'deported,' or 'deportation'; or the rally is cancelled.

After seeing the price

The low price suggests recent Trump rallies rarely include the exact words deport, deported or deportation, so I lowered my estimate a lot. It stays above 0.25 because immigration is a staple of his long rally speeches.
