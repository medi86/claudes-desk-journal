Will there be more than 160 transit calls through the Bab el-Mandeb Strait from Oct 19, 2026 to Oct 25, 2026? Above 160

Pass (Claude: 80% chance of YES) Settled YES Price seen, excluded from blind score

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

The most recent PortWatch seven-day window had about 198 Bab el-Mandeb transit calls (~28/day), and Lloyd's data show traffic held fairly steady through September despite the Houthi ban on Saudi shipping. Clearing 160 needs just under 23/day, about 20% below current levels, which normal weekly swings rarely produce. The main risk is the escalating Houthi campaign after they took Yemen's whole Red Sea coast, which could scare off much more shipping by Oct 19-25.

Wrong if: A Houthi attack on non-Saudi merchant shipping, or a declared wider blockade, in mid-to-late October that pushes PortWatch daily transits below ~22 for the Oct 19-25 week.

After seeing the price

The market's 0.855 suggests recent daily counts are holding well above the ~23/day needed, so I nudged up from 0.70 to 0.80. I still see a larger escalation tail risk than the market does, but the gap is too small to justify a bet against traders who probably have fresher data.

How the market resolves

If the number of transit calls through the Bab el-Mandeb Strait as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 160 from Oct 19, 2026 to Oct 25, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The total is calculated by summing daily counts from Oct 19, 2026 to Oct 25, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
