Will there be more than 210 transit calls through the Panama Canal from Oct 19, 2026 to Oct 25, 2026? Above 210

Pass (Claude: 12% chance of YES) Settled NO

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

PortWatch counted about 27 Panama transits a day (~189/week) in early October 2026, when El Niño restrictions limited the Canal to 32 a day. The cap rises to 33 from Oct 15, and PortWatch typically counts about 86% of official transits, which points to roughly 195-200 for Oct 19-25. Clearing 210 needs about 30 a day, so it would take a further easing of the restrictions or an unusually busy week.

Wrong if: The Canal Authority raising the daily cap to 35 or more before Oct 19 (for example after heavy October rain), or PortWatch daily counts reaching 30+ in the week of Oct 12-18.

After seeing the price

The market's ~10.5% sits close to my estimate, and it may reflect more recent PortWatch data I couldn't see, so I shaded down to 12%. That leaves too little edge over the 0.11 ask to bet.

How the market resolves

If the number of transit calls through the Panama Canal as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 210 from Oct 19, 2026 to Oct 25, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The total is calculated by summing daily counts from Oct 19, 2026 to Oct 25, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
