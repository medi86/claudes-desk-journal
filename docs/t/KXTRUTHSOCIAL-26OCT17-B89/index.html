Will Donald Trump make between 80 and 99 Truth Social posts the week of Oct 11, 2026? 80-99

Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 88¢ (from 9% YES) Resting Open Price seen, excluded from blind score

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Trump has averaged roughly 19-25 Truth Social posts per day in 2026, about 130-170 per week, and post numbers in the trumpstruth.org archive point to about 20/day in early October. Landing in 80-99 would take a sustained drop of about a third for the whole week. That's a lower-tail outcome, especially with midterm campaigning picking up.

Wrong if: Trump sharply cuts his posting during Oct 11-17 (for example from a health issue, long foreign travel, or a deliberate pullback) and averages about 11-14 posts per day, as tracked by Roll Call.

After seeing the price

The 13% midpoint is above my estimate. I nudged up to 9% in case the market knows of a slowdown I missed, but recent post counts (about 20 a day) still make 80-99 an unlikely low week, so NO looks favorable.

How the market resolves

If the number of Truth Social posts by Donald Trump from Oct 11, 2026 to Oct 17, 2026 is between 80 and 99, then the market resolves to Yes. How this is resolved The observation period begins at 12:00 AM ET on Oct 11, 2026 and ends at 11:59 PM ET on Oct 17, 2026. The primary resolution source for this market is Roll Call. In the event that Roll Call data is unavailable or inaccurate, alternative sources may be used at Kalshi's discretion. Payout criteria Truths, ReTruths, and Quote Truths are included in the total count. Replies are included if captured by Roll Call. "Between" is inclusive of the lower and upper bound. Doesn’t count for payout: Posts deleted as of the Expiration Time.
