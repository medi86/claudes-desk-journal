Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

The Rock Hill rally is confirmed for Oct 29 and nothing suggests it has been cancelled. Trump's October midterm push is built around immigration, and he said 'deported' at his Lubbock rally on Oct 27. Over a typical hour-plus rally speech he very likely says one of the counted forms. The main risks are wording like 'removed' or 'got them out' instead, or a short midday speech.

Wrong if: The Rock Hill transcript or video shows Trump talking about immigration only with words like 'removed,' 'got them out' or 'sent back' and never saying deport, deported or deportation, or the rally is cancelled.

After seeing the price

The price is far below my prior, which suggests the rally is underway or done without the word, or that traders know of a recent change in Trump's wording. I lowered my estimate sharply and am passing rather than betting against likely better-informed traders.
