Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Choosin' Texas probably got one October No. 1 week (the chart dated Oct 3) before Taylor Swift's 'Patient Zero' took the Oct 10, 17 and 24 charts. To get two October weeks it would have to win back No. 1 on the Oct 31 chart. That's unlikely for an old song against Swift's strong sales, and one Billboard snippet even mentions a fourth week for 'Patient Zero'.

Wrong if: Billboard's chart dated Oct 31, 2026 (released around Oct 27) shows 'Choosin' Texas' back at No. 1, or the market counts both the Oct 3 chart and another October-dated chart as Langley weeks.

After seeing the price

Swift's 'Patient Zero' holds the Oct 10, 17 and 24 charts, so Langley needs her likely Oct 3 week plus a comeback on the Oct 31 chart. That is unlikely, and if weeks are counted by release date even Oct 3 may not count. The 23% midpoint looks too high, so I'm betting NO.

How the market resolves
