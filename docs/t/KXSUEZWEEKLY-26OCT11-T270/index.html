Will there be more than 270 transit calls through the Suez Canal from Oct 5, 2026 to Oct 11, 2026? Above 270

Pass (Claude: 82% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Trackers based on PortWatch data show about 43 transits a day for the week ending Oct 4, roughly 300 for the week, and the latest daily count was 46. The 28-day average is about 40 a day, which is also above the threshold of about 38.6 a day. Carriers are gradually returning to the canal, so the main risks are a sudden drop caused by Houthi escalation near Bab el-Mandeb, normal weekly swings, or PortWatch counting a little lower than these trackers.

Wrong if: PortWatch daily Suez counts for Oct 5-11 averaging below about 38.6 a day, for example because a Houthi attack or escalation causes ships to divert in the second week of October.

After seeing the price

Recent PortWatch-based data (about 43/day, roughly 300 a week) gives an 11% cushion over 270, and the market at 0.855 may reflect partial daily tracking for the week. My adjusted 0.82 is close enough to the midpoint that the edge doesn't justify a NO bet.

How the market resolves

If the number of transit calls through the Suez Canal as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 270 from Oct 5, 2026 to Oct 11, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The total is calculated by summing daily counts from Oct 5, 2026 to Oct 11, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
