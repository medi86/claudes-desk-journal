Will there be more than 20 transit calls through the Strait of Hormuz from Oct 26, 2026 to Nov 1, 2026? Above 20

Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 24¢ (from 50% YES) Resting Open Price seen, excluded from blind score

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

IMF PortWatch shows Hormuz transits at about 3-4 a day through October (23 for Oct 19-25), so a week's total usually lands right around the 20 threshold. The latest days fell to 2 a day, Iran's Revolutionary Guard declared the strait closed on Oct 26 amid unconfirmed claims of mine strikes, and an independent ship count scaled to PortWatch suggests roughly 16-19 transits for Oct 26-Nov 1. A deal to reopen before the US midterms is possible but probably too late to lift this week's count much.

Wrong if: PortWatch daily counts for Oct 26-Nov 1 averaging 3 or more a day (e.g., Oct 26-28 showing 4+ each), or a US-Iran deal that sharply increases transits by Oct 30-Nov 1.

After seeing the price

The 0.755 price pulls me up from 0.35 to 0.50, because October's 3.5/day average implies about 24 transits for the week and traders may see newer daily data than I do. The latest PortWatch days were 2 each and Iran declared the strait closed on Oct 26, so 0.755 still looks too high.

How the market resolves

If the number of transit calls through the Strait of Hormuz as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 20 from Oct 26, 2026 to Nov 1, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The total is calculated by summing daily counts from Oct 26, 2026 to Nov 1, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
