What will Donald Trump say during Midterm Rally in Bullhead City, Arizona originally scheduled for October 14, 2026? Colorado River

Pass (Claude: 37% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

The rally sits right on the Colorado River, and the administration's large cuts to Arizona's river water are a big issue in the state this year, so Trump might name the river while riffing on the location or on water. But the cuts hurt his own side, so he may steer clear. He could also say only 'the river' or 'water', and his standard midterm speech doesn't need the topic. A single mention of the exact phrase is enough, so this is close to a coin flip, leaning slightly toward NO.

Wrong if: A transcript or video of the October 14 Bullhead City speech in which Trump says 'Colorado River' (for example, about water cuts, boaters or the view), or a cancellation that resolves the market as 'Event does not qualify'.

After seeing the price

The market at 0.345 is close to my independent estimate of about 0.40, so I'm shading down slightly. The remaining edge over the ask is only about 2 cents, too small to justify a trade given how uncertain I am.
