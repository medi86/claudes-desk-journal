Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Compass's August 5, 2026 release says the rolling NDA is underway and final submission is expected in Q4 2026, with launch in H1 2027. YES needs the submission finished in October, the first month of that window, and companies more often hit the middle or end of a guided quarter, or slip. The H1 2027 launch target gives a modest push toward an early-Q4 filing, and there is a small chance the resolver counts the rolling submission loosely.

Wrong if: Compass announces in October 2026 that it has completed its NDA submission for COMP360 to the FDA.

After seeing the price

The 14% price is well below my 28%, and since I couldn't check news after August or confirm the current date, the market likely reflects later information such as no October filing yet. I moved most of the way toward it, and the remaining 4-point edge is too thin to bet on.
