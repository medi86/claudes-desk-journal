Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

SpaceX averages roughly 12 launches a month in 2026, so more than 10 is normally likely. But October started slowly: about 5 or 6 launches by mid-month, after a Starlink Falcon 9 gap from Sept 19 to Oct 12. It now needs about 5 or 6 more flights in the last 13 days, which is achievable but slightly less likely than not given the thin manifest.

Wrong if: Starlink missions resume at near-daily pace from both coasts in late October (plus Bandwagon-5 and any Starship flight), bringing the October total to 11 or more.

After seeing the price

The 0.685 midpoint pushes me up from 0.38, since traders may have a more complete October count than my incomplete search. My best evidence still shows only about 5 to 6 launches by mid-month after a long Starlink pause, so 11 or more looks closer to a coin flip than 69%.
