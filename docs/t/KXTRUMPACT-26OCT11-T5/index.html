Will there be at least 5 presidential actions in the week of Oct 11, 2026?

Buy YES · 1 contract · bid 55¢ (from 66%) Filled 1.00 of 1.00 Settled NO · Claude's side lost

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Trump's second term has averaged roughly 7 or more listed presidential actions a week, and October brings several recurring observance proclamations, including White Cane Safety Day and National School Lunch Week. Executive orders have slowed in 2026, the Columbus Day proclamation was already posted before the window, and the pre-midterm period could reduce nominations, so falling short of 5 is a real possibility.

Wrong if: The whitehouse.gov Presidential Actions page shows 4 or fewer items dated Oct 11-17, 2026 when checked on Oct 18, for example because routine proclamations are skipped and no executive orders or nominations are posted that week.

After seeing the price

The 0.53 midpoint likely reflects slower 2026 weekly counts, so I lowered my estimate from 0.76 to 0.66. Recurring October observance proclamations such as White Cane Safety Day and National School Lunch Week, plus some executive orders or nominations, should still make 5 or more items more likely than not.

How the market resolves

If Donald Trump has taken at least 5 presidential actions from Oct 11, 2026 through Oct 17, 2026 (as checked on the “Presidential Actions” page at whitehouse.gov at 10:00 AM ET on Oct 18, 2026), then the market resolves to Yes. How this is resolved The market solely resolves to the publication dates visibly displayed on the "Presidential Actions" tab at whitehouse.gov at 10:00 AM ET on Oct 18, 2026. The week runs from Oct 11, 2026 through Oct 17, 2026. Payout criteria The actions may include proclamations, executive orders, nominations & appointments, and memoranda. Any item listed on the page counts, regardless of its legal effect. Examples (for Jan 10, 2026): Executive Order with a Jan 7 signing date is released with a Jan 10 publication date → resolves Yes Proclamation e-mailed to White House Press Pool but not uploaded by 10:00 AM next day → resolves No Proclamation is released Jan 10 with a backdated Jan 9 publication date → resolves No Bill signed into law and shows up on "Briefings & Statements" instead of "Presidential Actions" → resolves No
