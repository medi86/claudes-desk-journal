Will the #1 Show on Netflix have at least 12 million views?

Pass (Claude: 27% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

The current English TV leaders are weak: Below fell to 7.7M in its third week and Nobody Wants This S3 opened with 6.2M. The likely #1 for Oct 26–Nov 1 is the new docuseries 'The One About Matthew Perry', which was #2 on FlixPatrol's daily chart on Oct 29 behind the non-English Lupin. A celebrity docuseries in a partial debut week can reach 12M, but that is above the typical outcome, so YES is less likely than not.

Wrong if: Netflix's Nov 3 chart shows 'The One About Matthew Perry' (or another new English series) at #1 with 12M+ views.

After seeing the price

The thin orderbook pulled my estimate down slightly, from 0.32 to 0.27. That sits above the 0.195 midpoint but at or below the 0.28 ask, so buying YES has little edge given my uncertainty about the Matthew Perry docuseries' release timing and format.

How the market resolves

If the #1 Show on Netflix has at least 12 million views on the chart published on Nov 3, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. This market specifically encompasses the number of “Views” (not hours viewed) of the top Show ranked on Netflix’s weekly charts for Global "Shows | English" chart. The Netflix charts are updated on Tuesday. Therefore, the market will close at 11:59 PM ET the day before. The chart published on Nov 3, 2026 will be for the week ending the previous Sunday.
