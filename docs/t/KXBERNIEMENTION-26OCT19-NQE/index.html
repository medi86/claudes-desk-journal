Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Same-day local reporting (Oct 19, 2026) confirms the Amarillo Civic Center rally is on, with the program starting at 1 p.m., and the tour has run on schedule in Tampa and Gainesville over the prior two days. Sanders' rallies are usually livestreamed, so a qualifying video record is likely. The leftover risk is a last-minute no-show or remote-only appearance (as at the June 2025 Amarillo stop) or a lack of usable stream footage.

Wrong if: Reports that Sanders cancelled or missed the Amarillo rally, or spoke only by phone and Kalshi ruled that doesn't count, or that no live broadcast/stream of the event exists.

After seeing the price

A 0.895 midpoint on 'does not qualify' strongly suggests traders know the rally lacked a qualifying stream or Sanders didn't appear, which I couldn't check. I've updated sharply toward the market but have no independent edge to bet either way.

Payout Criteria The exact phrase/word, or a plural or possessive form of the phrase/word. Plural nouns occurring within proper nouns and noun phrases (e.g. names, titles, etc). Bernie Sanders speaking in an official capacity. For phrases with slashes like "Doge/Dogecoin," either word satisfies the criterion. Doesn't count for payout Grammatical/tense inflections are otherwise not included. Presence alone during a livestream is not sufficient for the market to qualify (e.g., inaudible private conversations on camera, unofficial statements picked up on a hot mic). Except as otherwise specified, previously aired content, archival footage, and recordings captured at times, places, or events wholly separate from the featured address. Payout for cancellations and delays If an event is definitively cancelled or the event fails to qualify under the Payout Criterion, or any other circumstance prevents normal resolution, then the market for "Event does not qualify" resolves to Yes and all other markets will resolve to No. If the event is postponed, and such a postponement is announced by the end of the following calendar day to be rescheduled to a time within 14 calendar days, the markets shall remain open.
