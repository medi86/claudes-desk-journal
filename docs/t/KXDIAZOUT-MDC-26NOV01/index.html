Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

As of Oct 22, 2026, Díaz-Canel holds both posts and is working normally (radio show, UN-vote diplomacy). He has publicly refused to resign despite heavy US pressure, sanctions and the indictment of Raúl Castro. With only about nine days left, YES would need a sudden shock such as US military action, an internal ouster, or a surprise announcement that he will leave within a year. Cuba's leadership rarely changes abruptly, so that's unlikely.

Wrong if: Before Nov 1, 2026, the Cuban government announces that Díaz-Canel is resigning or being replaced as President or First Secretary (or will be within a year), or a US operation removes him from power.

After seeing the price

The 11% midpoint likely reflects general fear of a US move or a sudden reshuffle rather than any concrete sign. With nine days left and Díaz-Canel visibly governing and refusing to resign, I nudge my estimate up only slightly to 4%, still well below the market.
