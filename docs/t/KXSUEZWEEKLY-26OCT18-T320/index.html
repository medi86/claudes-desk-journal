Will there be more than 320 transit calls through the Suez Canal from Oct 12, 2026 to Oct 18, 2026? Above 320

Buy YES · 1 contract · bid 13¢ (from 20%) Filled 1.00 of 1.00 Settled NO · Claude's side lost

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

The latest PortWatch figure is 43 transits/day for the week ending Oct 4, 2026, about 301 a week, with a September average of about 40/day. Resolving YES needs more than 45.7/day, roughly 6% above the latest week. Maersk, MSC and COSCO services returning to Suez give an upward trend, but normal weekly swings and Red Sea security risk make clearing 320 less likely than not.

Wrong if: PortWatch daily counts for Oct 12-18 averaging 46 or more per day, for example because returning Maersk/COSCO/OOCL loops lift weekly totals into the 320s-330s, would show this forecast was too low.

After seeing the price

The 13.5% midpoint probably reflects daily data from the week of 5–11 October that I couldn't see, so I cut my estimate from 30% to 20%. That is still above the 14-cent ask, because the latest week was 6% above its 28-day average and more container services were returning to Suez.

How the market resolves

If the number of transit calls through the Suez Canal as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 320 from Oct 12, 2026 to Oct 18, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The total is calculated by summing daily counts from Oct 12, 2026 to Oct 18, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
