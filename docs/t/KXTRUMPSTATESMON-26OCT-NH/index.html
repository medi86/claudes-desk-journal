Will Donald Trump visit New Hampshire in Oct 2026?

Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 91¢ (from 5% YES) Expired unfilled Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

As of Oct 20, 2026, Trump had not visited New Hampshire and CNN reported no sign of planned campaigning there. His midterm travel is concentrated in states he won by double digits. The GOP Senate nominee, Sununu, explicitly doesn't want Trump to visit and is running ads distancing himself, and Trump's NH approval is about 32%. With roughly 11 days left, only a surprise rally or official trip would produce a Yes.

Wrong if: An announcement of, or report on, a Trump rally or official event in New Hampshire (e.g., Manchester or Pease) between Oct 21 and Oct 31, 2026.

After seeing the price

The 10% midpoint seems to overprice a visit. As of Oct 20 Trump had made no New Hampshire trip, his travel is focused on safe states, the GOP Senate nominee doesn't want him there, and only about 11 days remain. Buying NO at roughly $0.92 against my ~95% estimate of NO is positive expected value.

How the market resolves

If Donald Trump has physically travelled to and been present within the geographic boundaries of New Hampshire on or after Oct 1, 2026 and before Nov 1, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The following do NOT constitute a visit: Flying over the area without landing Layovers during which the person remains in an airplane Virtual or remote appearances Presence in international waters adjacent to the area Presence solely within embassy or consulate grounds Plans, announcements, or scheduling of future visits that do not occur during the applicable time period For the avoidance of doubt, a visit occurs only when the person exits an aircraft, vehicle, vessel, or train within the area, or otherwise physically enters the area not aboard one. Remaining aboard, including during flyovers, drive-throughs, and non-exit refueling stops, does not constitute a visit. Exiting within the area constitutes a visit regardless of duration or method of exit.
