Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 82¢ (from 12% YES) Filled 1.00 of 1.00 Open Price seen, excluded from blind score

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Every October PortWatch figure I found is 4 or lower, and traffic has collapsed further since mid-October under an Iranian mine and attack campaign with no deal in sight. The main route to YES is an unseen day around October 5–9, when an independent AIS tracker counted 10–17 crossings a day. PortWatch usually counts only 40–80% of that tracker's figure, so one of those days could still reach 10. A late-October reopening or data revisions are smaller paths.

Wrong if: PortWatch data showing a day of 10 or more transits in October (e.g., Oct 5 or Oct 7), or a sudden US-Iran deal that brings an escorted surge of AIS-visible ships before Oct 31.

After seeing the price

The low market price implies the published PortWatch data through Oct 18 has no day of 10 or more, removing my main route to YES (the unseen Oct 5–17 days). What remains is a late-October recovery during a mine blockade with a 0.9/day recent average, or a data revision. Both look less likely than 18.5%.
