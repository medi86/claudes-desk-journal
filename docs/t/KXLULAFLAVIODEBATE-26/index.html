Will Lula and Flávio Bolsonaro debate before the Brazil presidential runoff? Before Oct 25, 2026

Pass (Claude: 80% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

The runoff is Lula vs. Flávio Bolsonaro, and both have publicly said they'll debate. Band (Oct 18) and Globo (Oct 23) debates are scheduled, and SBT has one planned. Brazilian runoff debates between the two finalists usually happen, as they did in 2022. The risks are that both skipped every first-round debate, the frontrunner Flávio might pull out, and Record already cancelled the Oct 11 debate over 'schedule incompatibility.'

Wrong if: Lula or Flávio pulls out of both the Band Oct 18 and Globo Oct 23 debates (and any SBT date), so the runoff ends with no head-to-head debate.

After seeing the price

The market's 0.815 midpoint nearly matches my independent 0.80 estimate, so the price doesn't change my view. The gap is too small to justify a bet given the Record cancellation and both candidates' history of skipping debates.

How the market resolves

If Lula and Flávio Bolsonaro have a debate before Oct 25, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. A debate counts only if both candidates are present at the same time, in person or via live connection; the event is announced as a debate or similar confrontational discussion format; the candidates respond to each other's positions through direct dialogue or moderated questions; and the event is intended for public viewing. Joint press conferences, sequential interviews or town halls where the candidates appear separately, panel discussions with more than two primary participants, and events where either candidate fails to appear or appears only by pre-recorded video do not count.
