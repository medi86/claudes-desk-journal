Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

MAGA Inc. had $415.8M at the end of August after raising $23.7M and spending only $11.4M that month. To end September at $325M or less, it would need to spend or transfer roughly $110M gross in one month, about ten times its August pace. The September spending made public so far, including $15M on the Texas Senate race, is small, though Trump's pledge of $400–500M for the midterms leaves room for large late-September transfers.

Wrong if: The October monthly FEC report (covering September) shows transfers to allied super PACs or prepaid ad buys of about $100M or more, pushing cash on hand to $325M or below.

After seeing the price

The market midpoint of 0.81 nearly matches my 0.82 estimate, so the price does not change my view. With a 0.77/0.85 spread, buying YES at 0.85 or NO at the 0.77 bid would both be negative expected value.
