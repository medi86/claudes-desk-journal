Will the National Republican Senatorial Committee report more than $45M in cash on hand at the end of Q3? Above $45M

Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 86¢ (from 6% YES) Resting Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

The NRSC had a record $48.8M on hand at the end of May 2026. The FEC's committee overview page shows ending cash of about $18.7M for the period through 08/31/2026, so the committee has been spending down heavily ahead of the midterms. Getting back above $45M by September 30 would mean adding about $26M+ net in one heavy-spending month, far beyond its recent monthly fundraising of about $8–10M. The main remaining risk is that I couldn't confirm the $18.7M figure from a second source.

Wrong if: The NRSC's October 20 FEC monthly report shows cash on hand above $45M as of September 30, 2026. That could happen through a very large transfer from joint fundraising committees, or because the $18.7M end-of-August figure was wrong.

After seeing the price

The market at about 15% also sees YES as unlikely; the gap likely reflects doubt about the end-of-August figure. If cash really was about $18.7M then, rebuilding to over $45M in one September of heavy spending is very unlikely, so I nudge up to 6% but still bet NO.

How the market resolves

If the National Republican Senatorial Committee's cash on hand is above $45 million as of September 30, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. This market resolves based on the official filing data released by the Federal Election Commission (FEC). Only the first official or non-preliminary release of the relevant statistic will be used to resolve the market, and subsequent revisions, corrections, or updates will not affect resolution.
