Will Ramp AI Index OpenAI model adoption rate for September 2026 be above 40.3%?

Pass (Claude: 13% chance of YES) Settled YES

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

OpenAI's Ramp adoption rate was 39.76% in August 2026. Since Ramp's mid-2026 method change, the monthly moves have been -0.1, +0.23 and +0.09 points, so clearing 40.3% needs about a +0.55-point jump, roughly double the best recent month. Price cuts and record provider switching in September give some upside. But overall adoption growth is slowing, and late summer has historically been flat, so a jump that size is unlikely.

Wrong if: Ramp's October 2026 AI Index release (or its weekly dashboard) reporting OpenAI's September adoption at 40.4% or higher, for example because price cuts and new GPT models brought a burst of new business sign-ups.

After seeing the price

The 10% midpoint likely reflects September dashboard data or an early October release that I couldn't verify, so I lowered my estimate from 20% to 13%. The remaining gap over the 11-cent ask isn't worth betting on.
