When will SpaceX launch Dragon CRS-35? Before Oct 16, 2026

Pass (Claude: 80% chance of YES) Settled YES

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

SpaceX's official mission page targets CRS-35 for Oct 13 at 6:33 a.m. ET, with a backup on Oct 14. The market deadline is 11:59 p.m. ET on Oct 15, so there are about 2-3 launch opportunities. Cargo Dragon launches often slip a few days for weather or technical reasons (CRS-34 needed three attempts over four days), so a slip past the narrow window is a real risk.

Wrong if: An announced delay of CRS-35 to October 16 or later (e.g., for an ISS schedule conflict, a vehicle issue or bad weather), or scrubs on Oct 13, 14 and 15.

After seeing the price

The market's 0.87 likely reflects fresher information (weather forecasts, launch-day status) that I couldn't see, so I moved up from 0.66 to 0.80. The remaining gap is too small to justify a NO bet against a probably better-informed price.

How the market resolves
