Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Trump averaged about 18 posts a day in his first year back in office, and his record month (May 2026, 861 posts) works out to only about 190 a week. Hitting 360 in seven days needs about 51 a day, nearly double that record pace. Election week and his history of 150+ post late-night sprees make a spike plausible, but 360 would still be extreme.

Wrong if: Roll Call's Factba.se tracker shows one or more 100+ post sprees around Election Day (Nov 3) and a running total above about 200 by Nov 4.

After seeing the price

The market's 0.375 midpoint suggests recent weekly counts may be near 360, data I didn't find, so I moved up from 17% to 27%. That is still below the midpoint because 360 needs about 51 posts a day, nearly double his record monthly pace.

How the market resolves

If the number of Truth Social posts by Donald Trump from Nov 1, 2026 to Nov 7, 2026 is at least 360, then the market resolves to Yes. How this is resolved The observation period begins at 12:00:00 AM ET on Nov 1, 2026 and ends at 11:59:59 PM ET on Nov 7, 2026. The primary resolution source for this market is Roll Call. In the event that Roll Call data is unavailable or inaccurate, alternative sources may be used at Kalshi's discretion. Payout criteria Truths, ReTruths, and Quote Truths are included in the total count. Replies are included if captured by Roll Call. "Between" is inclusive of the lower and upper bound. Doesn’t count for payout: Posts deleted as of 12:00 AM ET.
