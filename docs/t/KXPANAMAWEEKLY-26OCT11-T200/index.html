Will there be more than 200 transit calls through the Panama Canal from Oct 5, 2026 to Oct 11, 2026? Above 200

Pass (Claude: 22% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

From Sept 15 to Oct 14, 2026 the Panama Canal Authority caps transits at 32 a day because of El Niño drought, so Oct 5–11 allows at most about 224 transits. Demand is well above that (queues of 100+ ships, waits over a week), so actual transits should be about 31–32 a day. The threshold of 200 needs about 28.6 a day on PortWatch's satellite-based count, which likely but not certainly matches the authority's figures. Clearing it is more likely than not but not secure.

Wrong if: PortWatch's daily Panama counts for late September and early October 2026 averaging below about 28 a day (under about 196 a week) despite the 32-per-day cap, or an emergency tightening or disruption of operations during Oct 5–11.

After seeing the price

My 66% assumed PortWatch's count roughly matches the canal authority's, which I couldn't verify. The 17.5% price suggests traders watching PortWatch see recent capped weeks below 200, so I revise down heavily. Little edge remains against the ask.

How the market resolves

If the number of transit calls through the Panama Canal as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 200 from Oct 5, 2026 to Oct 11, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The total is calculated by summing daily counts from Oct 5, 2026 to Oct 11, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
