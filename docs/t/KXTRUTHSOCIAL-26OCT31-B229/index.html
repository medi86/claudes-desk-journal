Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Trump's 2026 baseline is about 19 posts/day (~133/week), and his record month of May averaged 27/day (~189/week). The 220-239 range needs ~31-34/day for the whole week. The final pre-midterm week and his habit of 100+ post late-night binges give real upside, but this narrow range is still above the most likely outcome.

Wrong if: Roll Call's count for Oct 25-31 shows a late-night binge or a midterm-eve surge that pushes the weekly total to 220-239.

After seeing the price

I moved partway toward the 13% midpoint because traders can see the live mid-week count and I couldn't. The remaining gap is too small to bet NO against people with better information on the week so far.

How the market resolves
