Will there be more than 180 transit calls through the Panama Canal from Oct 26, 2026 to Nov 1, 2026? Above 180

Pass (Claude: 80% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

IMF PortWatch recorded about 27 transits a day in the week ending Oct 4, 2026 (~189 for the week) and about 26 a day in the week to Oct 11 (~182), both close to the 180 threshold. The canal authority raised daily capacity from 32 to 33 slots on Oct 15 as water levels recovered, which points to about 186-195 PortWatch transits for Oct 26-Nov 1. Normal week-to-week swings of about 10-12 transits and weak tanker traffic still leave a real chance of finishing at 180 or below.

Wrong if: PortWatch daily counts for Oct 26-Nov 1 averaging 25.7 or less (sum of 180 or below), for example from a slow week, weather or operational problems, or AIS data gaps.

After seeing the price

The market's 87.5% likely reflects PortWatch days after the October 15 capacity increase that I couldn't see; my latest data (about 182–189 a week in early October) is stale. I moved up to 80% but don't trust my information enough to bet NO against the market.

How the market resolves

If the number of transit calls through the Panama Canal as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 180 from Oct 26, 2026 to Nov 1, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The total is calculated by summing daily counts from Oct 26, 2026 to Nov 1, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
