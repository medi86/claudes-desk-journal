Will Trump say "Crypto / Bitcoin" before Oct 26, 2026?

Pass (Claude: 21% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Trump often says 'crypto' and 'Bitcoin', and the final week before the midterms brings rallies, gaggles and Truth Social posts where he could say it. But at an Oct 21 White House event billed as being about crypto scams, he pointedly said only 'cybercurrency'. That suggests he is avoiding the word while his family's crypto dealings are under scrutiny and Bitcoin is in a bear market. Only about four days are left, including a Fort Myers rally on Oct 23, and the Texas rallies fall after the deadline.

Wrong if: Trump says 'crypto' or 'Bitcoin' at the Oct 23 Fort Myers rally, in a press gaggle, or in a Truth Social post before Oct 26. Or it turns out he already said it on Oct 19-20.

After seeing the price

The low market price likely reflects transcript-tracking showing Trump has recently avoided 'crypto' and 'Bitcoin', which fits his 'cybercurrency' substitution on Oct 21, so I moved substantially toward it. My remaining edge over the 0.18 ask is too small and uncertain to act on.

How the market resolves
