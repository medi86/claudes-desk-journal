Will President Trump sign at least 20 bills into law in Oct 2026?

Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 39¢ (from 40% YES) Expired unfilled Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Official White House statements and trackers show 18 bills signed in October 2026 (Oct 7: 2, Oct 9: 1, Oct 15: 3, Oct 20: 10, Oct 22: 2), with nothing more listed through Oct 26. Congress is in pre-election recess, so YES needs at least two more already-passed bills to be sent and signed between Oct 27 and 31. The latest batch was small, three weeks after Congress left, which suggests the backlog is nearly gone, though another wave is possible.

Wrong if: A White House 'Congressional Bills ... Signed into Law' statement dated Oct 27-31, 2026 listing two or more bills.

After seeing the price

The 61.5% market price hints at information I lack, likely already-passed bills awaiting signature, so I moved up from 24% to 40%. That is still well below the midpoint, given only 18 confirmed signings, no new signings Oct 23-26, and Congress in recess.

How the market resolves

If the President signs above 19 bills into law in Oct 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. Joint resolutions are included.
