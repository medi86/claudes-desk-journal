The 20% midpoint pulled me up slightly, since my research was limited and Obama's two dozen unreleased ads could include Peltola. With no reported endorsement, a few days left, and Peltola likely preferring distance, I still see YES below the market price.

How the market resolves

If Barack Obama publicly endorses Mary Peltola in Alaska Senate before Nov 3, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. An endorsement requires a clear, affirmative statement of support for the nominee's election. The statement must be public (not private communications) and specifically reference the race or be clearly in context of the race. The endorsement must come directly from the person or through their official channels. Positive comments without explicit support, and policy endorsements without endorsement of the candidate, do not qualify. An endorsement conditioned only on the candidate declaring candidacy for, filing for, entering, or remaining in the race qualifies once the statement is confirmed, whether or not the candidate later does so. An endorsement subject to any other condition does not qualify. Social media endorsements count only from verified accounts, and likes or retweets alone do not constitute endorsement. Quote tweets with additional supportive commentary do count. Sarcastic statements or those immediately clarified as jokes do not qualify.
