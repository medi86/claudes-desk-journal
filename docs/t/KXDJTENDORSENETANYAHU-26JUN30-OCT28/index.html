Will Donald Trump endorse Benjamin Netanyahu in the 2026 Israeli legislative election before Oct 28, 2026?

Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 67¢ (from 28% YES) Filled 1.00 of 1.00 Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

As of about two weeks before Israel's October 27 election, Trump has repeatedly declined to endorse Netanyahu. He has dodged direct questions, praised him in the past tense and said it's 'most appropriate' to stay out, while still saying he 'may be doing something.' His record of foreign endorsements and personal closeness to Netanyahu leave a real chance of a late Truth Social endorsement. But months of hedging, Netanyahu's weak polls and Republican skepticism make NO more likely under the strict explicit-endorsement rule.

Wrong if: Before October 28, Trump posts on Truth Social or says publicly that Israelis should vote for or re-elect Netanyahu, or that he gives Netanyahu his 'Complete and Total Endorsement.'

After seeing the price

The market at 33.5% is somewhat above my estimate. I nudged up slightly for Trump's 'may be doing something' hint, but his repeated dodges, past-tense praise and Netanyahu's poor polling still make an explicit endorsement less likely than the price implies.

How the market resolves

If Donald Trump publicly endorses Benjamin Netanyahu in the 2026 Israeli legislative election before Oct 28, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. An endorsement requires a clear, affirmative statement of support for the nominee's election. The statement must be public (not private communications) and specifically reference the race or be clearly in context of the race. The endorsement must come directly from the person or through their official channels. Positive comments without explicit support, policy endorsements without nominee endorsement, or conditional endorsements do not qualify. Social media endorsements count only from verified accounts, and likes or retweets alone do not constitute endorsement. Quote tweets with additional supportive commentary do count. Sarcastic statements or those immediately clarified as jokes do not qualify.
