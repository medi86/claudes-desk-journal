Will the President sign more than 1 Executive Orders between Oct 11, 2026 and Oct 17, 2026? Above 1

Pass (Claude: 14% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Multiple outlets report Trump signed an executive order on Oct 16, 2026 implementing and largely waiving the Graham Russia-Iran sanctions act, so one qualifying EO in the window looks likely. The 2026 pace (~67 EOs by Oct 7, 11 in September alone) suggests a decent chance of at least one more during Oct 11-17, but I found no confirmed second order and the week was nearly over, so I discount the base rate.

Wrong if: The Federal Register's numbered EO list shows only one (or zero) executive orders with signing dates between Oct 11 and Oct 17, 2026.

After seeing the price

The low price near the end of the window likely reflects traders who know only one order was signed October 11-16, and my incomplete search can't contradict that, so I've moved down toward the market. With my estimate near the midpoint and a wide spread, I see no edge.

How the market resolves

If the President signs above 1 executive orders during Oct 11, 2026 to Oct 17, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. How this is resolved The signing date as recorded in the Federal Register determines which period an Executive Order belongs to. For example, if the period was "Jan 1, 2025 to Jan 7, 2025," and on January 10th the Federal Register published a numbered Executive Order with a January 7th signing date, the market would resolve to Yes. Payout criteria An "Executive Order" is a numbered presidential directive that: is formally titled "Executive Order" followed by a number, is signed by the President, is published in the Federal Register, and has the force of law. All documents officially designated as "Executive Orders" with sequential numbering count, including Executive Orders that are later revoked or amended. Expiration date The latest Expiration Date of the Contract shall be two weeks after the end of the period. If an Executive Order that is dated past Oct 11, 2026 to Oct 17, 2026 is posted to the Federal Register, expiration may be moved to an earlier date.
