The Cat in the Hat Rotten Tomatoes score? Above 65

Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 30¢ (from 53% YES) Resting Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

No critic reviews were on Rotten Tomatoes yet. Comparable films (the Seuss adaptations The Lorax at 54% and The Grinch at about 60%, plus big-studio animated films outside Pixar and Disney) land near 65% about half the time or less. Early fan reactions on Letterboxd are mixed, and the film was moved from early 2026 to November, so I lean slightly below even odds despite the directors' good track records and a likable lead.

Wrong if: If the embargo lifts with mostly positive reviews (for example, the Tomatometer opens in the 80s with 50+ reviews and holds through Nov 9), this forecast was too low; a score in the 40s-50s would confirm it.

After seeing the price

The market at 70% likely reflects positive post-premiere buzz, which pulls me up from 43%. That buzz is usually unreliable for predicting critics, and base rates for this type of film sit near the 65% threshold, so 70% looks too high.
