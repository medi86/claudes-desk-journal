Will Donald Trump meet (including phone calls) Volodymyr Zelenskyy in Oct 2026?

Pass (Claude: 37% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Trump and Zelenskyy last met at the UN on Sept. 22, and there is no reported direct contact in October so far. Relations are at their worst since the 2025 Oval Office clash: Trump has said someone else should negotiate for Ukraine and is dealing with Kyiv through Witkoff and Kushner. Still, the two leaders have historically spoken often, and the White House wants a peace breakthrough before the Nov. 3 midterms, so a call in the last ~12 days of October is quite possible but less likely than not.

Wrong if: A confirmed Trump–Zelenskyy phone call (bilateral or a group call with European leaders) or in-person meeting between Oct 20 and Oct 31, 2026, or evidence of one that already happened in early October.

After seeing the price

The market at 0.42 is somewhat above my estimate, and I couldn't rule out a contact in early October, so I moved from 0.35 to 0.37. The remaining edge against the 0.40 bid is too small to justify a bet on NO.

How the market resolves
