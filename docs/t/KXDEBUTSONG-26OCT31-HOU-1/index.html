Will Housewife by Tate McRae debut #1 on the Billboard Hot 100?

Pass (Claude: 13% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Tate McRae has never had a Hot 100 #1; her best singles ('Greedy', 'Tit for Tat') peaked at #3. 'Housewife' releases Thursday Oct 15, so the Oct 31 chart is likely its first full tracking week. It must beat Ella Langley's 'Choosin' Texas', which has repeatedly reclaimed #1, plus any new big-star releases. A lead single for a hyped new era, extra versions for sale and Victoria's Secret show exposure give it a real but minority chance.

Wrong if: Billboard's chart dated Oct 31, 2026 shows 'Housewife' at #1 (e.g., after a big first-week streaming total plus strong sales of extra versions while 'Choosin' Texas' fades and no major release competes).

After seeing the price

My 13% is close to the 15% midpoint, and the 9–21 cent spread means buying NO at about 91 cents would exceed my roughly 87% NO probability. The market price doesn't change my view that she probably falls short against 'Choosin' Texas' given her #3 career peak.
