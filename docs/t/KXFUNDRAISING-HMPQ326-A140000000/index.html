Will House Majority PAC report more than $140M in cash on hand at the end of Q3? Above $140M

Pass (Claude: 44% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

House Majority PAC had $135.2M cash on hand on Aug 31, 2026 per FEC data, so it needs roughly a $5M net gain in September to clear $140M. It reportedly announced a record $230M Q3 haul, implying large September receipts, but September is also when fall TV spending ramps up sharply. Inflows and outflows are both plausibly $40-80M, so the outcome is near a coin flip with a slight lean toward yes given the record fundraising.

Wrong if: HMP's October monthly FEC report (covering September) showing ending cash on hand at or below $140M because of heavy September ad spending.

After seeing the price

My 0.52 estimate rested on limited data: Aug 31 cash of $135.2M and only the headline of the record Q3 announcement. The market at 0.40 may reflect details of that announcement I couldn't see, so I moved toward it. The remaining edge over the 0.43 ask is too small to bet.
