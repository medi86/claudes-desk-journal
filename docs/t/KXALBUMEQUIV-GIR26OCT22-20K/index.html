Will The Girl Of Your Dreams by Ashe have above 20000 Album Equivalent Units during the October 16, 2026 - October 22, 2026 tracking week? Above 20K

Pass (Claude: 86% chance of YES) Settled YES

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Ashe's lead single 'Stop the Wedding!' just reached No. 34 on the Hot 100 and was still climbing when the album dropped Oct 16 on Atlantic, with 245K+ Spotify pre-saves and many physical variants and in-store events. A No. 34-level single plus first-week streams of the other tracks likely gives about 20-25K streaming units, and physical sales should add 8-20K, putting the total around 30-40K. The main risk is that her breakout is concentrated in the UK and Ireland, and her earlier albums barely charted in the U.S.

Wrong if: Luminate reports the album's debut week at or below 20,000 units, for example because U.S. album-track streaming is weak and physical sales come in under about 5K.

After seeing the price

The market sits above my 0.80 estimate, likely reflecting midweek sales projections I couldn't access, so I moved up to about 0.86. That is too close to the 0.89 midpoint, and inside the wide spread, to justify a NO bet.
