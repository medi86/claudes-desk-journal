Will Jon Ossoff for Senate report more than $30M in total receipts during Q3? Above $30M

Pass (Claude: 68% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Ossoff's campaign reported about $20M raised in Q2 2026, up from $14M in Q1 and about $12M per quarter in 2025. Top Democratic Senate candidates have typically raised 1.5 to 2.5 times their Q2 total in Q3 of an election year, so $30M (1.5x) is near the low end of that range. Things that could keep him below it: money shifting to other competitive races, his poll lead, and uncertainty over what the FEC counts as total receipts. I could not verify any announced Q3 figure.

Wrong if: The FEC Q3 report or a campaign announcement showing Ossoff's total receipts for July 1 to September 30, 2026 at or below $30M (e.g., a reported haul of around $25M).

After seeing the price

The market at 0.74 sits above my 0.62 extrapolation, possibly because traders know of an early-October Q3 announcement I couldn't see, so I moved partway toward it. The remaining gap is small next to the wide spread and my information disadvantage, so I pass.
