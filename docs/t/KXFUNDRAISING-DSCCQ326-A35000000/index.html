Will the Democratic Senatorial Campaign Committee report more than $35M in cash on hand at the end of Q3? Above $35M

Buy YES · 1 contract · bid 23¢ (from 27%) Resting Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

FEC data shows the DSCC's cash on hand falling from about $41.0M (June 30) to about $40M (July 31) to $37.6M (Aug 31), leaving only about $2.6M of room above $35M. In a competitive midterm, September usually brings a sharp rise in party-committee spending on fall ads and help for candidates, which tends to outpace strong end-of-quarter fundraising. Big September fundraising or large transfers in could still keep the balance above $35M.

Wrong if: The DSCC's September monthly report (due around Oct. 20) shows ending cash on hand above $35M, for example because record September fundraising or large joint-fundraising transfers outpaced fall ad spending.

After seeing the price

The market's 0.195 midpoint pulls me down from 0.35, since heavy September spending could easily erase the $2.6M cushion. But the wide spread and $37.6M at end of August still leave about a 1-in-4 chance, slightly above the 0.24 ask.

How the market resolves

If the Democratic Senatorial Campaign Committee's cash on hand is above $35 million as of September 30, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. This market resolves based on the official filing data released by the Federal Election Commission (FEC). Only the first official or non-preliminary release of the relevant statistic will be used to resolve the market, and subsequent revisions, corrections, or updates will not affect resolution.
