Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

The Lubbock rally is confirmed for the evening of Oct 27, 2026. Trump just called the Iran ceasefire a 'waste of time' at a Mississippi midterm rally, he recently announced a Russia–Ukraine 'energy ceasefire', and a Melania-brokered ceasefire in Oleshky started the same day. With three ceasefire stories in the news and his habit of long, freewheeling rally speeches that repeat stump material, saying the word at least once is very likely. The main risks are a cancellation or a shorter speech focused on domestic issues.

Wrong if: A full transcript or video of the Oct 27 Lubbock rally shows Trump never saying 'ceasefire', or the rally is cancelled.

After seeing the price

A 20% price on rally day probably means the speech is in progress or done without 'ceasefire' so far, so I'm cutting my estimate sharply. With three live ceasefire stories and his recent rally use of the word, I still put it slightly above the market, but with low confidence.
