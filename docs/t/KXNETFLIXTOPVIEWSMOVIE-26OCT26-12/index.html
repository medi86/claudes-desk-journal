Pass (Claude: 80% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

FlixPatrol shows Ben Affleck's Animals still dominating Netflix's global movie rankings on Oct 19 (859 points vs 298 for #2), which usually means a weekly total well above 12M even in a third chart week. Tyler Perry's Doing Life is a second possible top film, and the new Oct 21–23 releases look modest. Historically most weeks' #1 English film clears 12M, but weak weeks fall to 8–11M, and Animals will be losing viewers.

Wrong if: Netflix's Oct 27 chart shows the #1 English film (likely Animals or Doing Life) under 12.0M views because Animals dropped sharply in week three and no new release took off.

After seeing the price

The market at 0.845 is above my 0.74, and traders may know Animals' actual week-two views, which I couldn't find. I'm moving toward their price, and the remaining gap is too small and uncertain to bet NO.
