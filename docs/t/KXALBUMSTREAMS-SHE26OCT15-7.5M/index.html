Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Kworb's data shows 'She's the Best' at only about 83k Spotify streams a day worldwide, about 8.7M total since its August 14 release. That suggests US streams across all platforms of well under 1M a week before the album came out. Even a strong album-release bump in the October 9–15 week would likely leave it around 1–2M, far short of 7.5M.

Wrong if: Luminate reporting the song above 7.5M US streams for Oct 9–15, for example because of a viral TikTok moment, a major sync, or a sudden spike on the Hot 100 driven by the album launch.

After seeing the price

The high price suggests the market may be counting album streams, or traders know something I don't, so I moved up from 2%. Kworb's numbers for the song itself still point to roughly 1–2M US streams this week, so I still lean NO.
