Will it be reported by any of the Source Agencies that Russian-origin diesel fuel is imported into the United States before Nov 1, 2026?

Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 84¢ (from 9% YES) Expired unfilled Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

As of Oct 21, the first Russian diesel tanker bound for the US was only scheduled to leave port on Oct 22. Voyages from Russian ports to US ports usually take about 13-20+ days, so physical delivery before Nov 1 is very unlikely. On top of that, the congressional import ban remains in force, departure could slip, and a qualifying source would have to report the delivery in time.

Wrong if: A qualifying source reports that a tanker carrying Russian-refined diesel docked and discharged at a US port on or before Oct 31, 2026 (e.g., a cargo already at sea that was diverted, or a fast Arctic voyage).

After seeing the price

The 20% midpoint makes me allow a little for information I lack, such as a cargo already afloat being diverted. But the first announced tanker was only due to depart on Oct 22, and normal voyage times put its arrival in November. My estimate stays well below the market, so I bet NO.

How the market resolves

If Russian-origin diesel fuel is imported into the United States after Issuance and before Nov 1, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. Only reporting from any of the Source Agencies qualifies for the purposes of this market. Reports that merely reference prior publications (from before Issuance) do not qualify. Republishing of content originally produced by other sources (e.g., wire services) only qualifies if the content is hosted on the outlet's official platform and the outlet is clearly identified as the publishing entity. Russian-origin diesel fuel is diesel fuel produced in Russia. Diesel fuel refined in another country from Russian crude oil is not Russian-origin diesel fuel. Diesel fuel is imported into the United States when it is physically delivered to a U.S. port, or otherwise into U.S. territory, for commercial or government use within the United States. Whether the import is made under the October 9, 2026 agreement between the United States and Russia, General License 135 or any other authorization does not matter. The following do NOT satisfy the Payout Criterion: A purchase, contract, loading or voyage of Russian-origin diesel fuel to the United States, before it is actually delivered Diesel fuel carried as a vessel's own fuel Diesel fuel held in bond for re-export or passing through the United States in transit to another country An announcement, agreement or license permitting imports, before diesel fuel is actually delivered
