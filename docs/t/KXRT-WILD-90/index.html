Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Wild Horse Nine sits at 93% (88 fresh, 7 rotten) about two weeks before its Nov 6 wide release, and Metacritic shows 79 with zero negative reviews. Wide-release reviews usually pull festival scores down by 1-2 points, and recent reviews have been about 87% fresh, so the final score probably lands around 91-92%. That is only just above the 90 line.

Wrong if: A wave of mixed or negative US critic reviews at the Nov 6 release pushes the displayed Tomatometer to 90% or lower by Nov 9.

After seeing the price

The 0.875 price led me to raise my estimate somewhat, since films like Banshees held their festival scores. But at 92.6% the buffer above the 91 display threshold is only about 2 points, recent reviews are about 87% fresh, and release-week critics usually pull scores down. I still think the market overrates YES.
