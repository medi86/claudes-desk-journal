Will there be at least 3 presidential actions in the week of Oct 25, 2026?

Buy YES · 1 contract · bid 43¢ (from 68%) Resting Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

In 2026 the Trump White House has posted several proclamations and executive orders almost every week, including at least five proclamations and four executive orders in October alone. The last week of October usually brings proclamations for November observances, and a tariff proclamation on robotics and machinery appears to have been signed midweek. With midterms on Nov 3, a week with fewer than 3 listed actions would be unusual.

Wrong if: The whitehouse.gov Presidential Actions page on the morning of Nov 1, 2026 shows 2 or fewer items with publication dates between Oct 25 and Oct 31, for example because campaign travel slowed postings or November proclamations were posted late or backdated.

After seeing the price

The low market price hints that few or no items have been posted this week, so I cut my estimate from 0.95 to 0.68. Proclamations for November observances usually come Oct 30–31, and a robotics tariff proclamation was apparently signed midweek, so reaching 3 items still looks more likely than not.

How the market resolves

If Donald Trump has taken at least 3 presidential actions from Oct 25, 2026 through Oct 31, 2026 (as checked on the “Presidential Actions” page at whitehouse.gov at 10:00 AM ET on Nov 1, 2026), then the market resolves to Yes. How this is resolved The market solely resolves to the publication dates visibly displayed on the "Presidential Actions" tab at whitehouse.gov at 10:00 AM ET on Nov 1, 2026. The week runs from Oct 25, 2026 through Oct 31, 2026. Payout criteria The actions may include proclamations, executive orders, nominations & appointments, and memoranda. Any item listed on the page counts, regardless of its legal effect. Examples (for Jan 10, 2026): Executive Order with a Jan 7 signing date is released with a Jan 10 publication date → resolves Yes Proclamation e-mailed to White House Press Pool but not uploaded by 10:00 AM next day → resolves No Proclamation is released Jan 10 with a backdated Jan 9 publication date → resolves No Bill signed into law and shows up on "Briefings & Statements" instead of "Presidential Actions" → resolves No
