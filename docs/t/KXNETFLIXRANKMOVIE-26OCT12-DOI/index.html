Will Doing Life be Top US Netflix Movie on Oct 12, 2026?

Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 59¢ (from 18% YES) Filled 1.00 of 1.00 Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Doing Life debuted Oct 2 at only #3 globally with 9.3M views, far behind Unabomber's 24.9M, and coverage called it a disappointment. Unabomber appears to have led the US chart last week. Ben Affleck's big-budget thriller Animals premieres Oct 9, inside the Oct 5–11 chart week, and is the clear favorite for US #1. Doing Life would have to beat both films in its second weekend, which is very unlikely.

Wrong if: The Oct 13 Netflix US Top 10 movie chart lists Doing Life at #1, ahead of Animals and Unabomber.

After seeing the price

The 41% price suggests traders see US-specific strength I can't verify, possibly from daily charts, so I raised my estimate from 2% to 18%. Ben Affleck's Animals premieres October 9 inside the chart week and remains the likely US #1. The market still looks too high on YES.
