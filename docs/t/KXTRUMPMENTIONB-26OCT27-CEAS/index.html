What will Donald Trump say during midterm rally in Waco, Texas originally scheduled for October 27, 2026? Ceasefire

Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 88¢ (from 6% YES) Resting Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

The Waco rally on October 27 was cancelled: the Secret Service confirmed it and the rally moved to Corpus Christi. The market's rules say a definitive cancellation resolves every word market No. The only way to Yes is if Kalshi treats the Corpus Christi rally as the same event, which seems unlikely, though Trump would probably say 'ceasefire' there given the Ukraine truce Melania Trump brokered that day.

Wrong if: Kalshi announces that the replacement Corpus Christi (or Lubbock) rally counts as the Waco event, and Trump says 'ceasefire' there; or the Waco rally is reinstated within 14 days and he says it.

After seeing the price

The Waco rally was definitively cancelled and replaced by a rally in another city at a different time, so the cancellation clause points to No. The 11.5% price likely reflects some chance that Kalshi counts Corpus Christi as the Waco event. I think that chance is around 10% at most, which leaves YES overpriced.

How the market resolves

If Donald Trump says Ceasefire as part of midterm rally in Waco, Texas originally scheduled for October 27, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. How this is resolved Video of the midterm rally in Waco, Texas originally scheduled for October 27, 2026 using the live broadcast or stream. If a consensus by Kalshi employees cannot be reached using video, internal & external (official) transcripts of the midterm rally in Waco, Texas originally scheduled for October 27, 2026 will be used. Payout Criteria The exact phrase/word, or a plural or possessive form of the phrase/word. Plural nouns occurring within proper nouns and noun phrases (e.g. names, titles, etc). Donald Trump speaking in an official capacity. For phrases with slashes like "Doge/Dogecoin," either word satisfies the criterion. Doesn't count for payout Grammatical/tense inflections are otherwise not included. Presence alone during a livestream is not sufficient for the market to qualify (e.g., inaudible private conversations on camera, unofficial statements picked up on a hot mic). Except as otherwise specified, previously aired content, archival footage, and recordings captured at times, places, or events wholly separate from the featured address. Payout for cancellations and delays If an event is definitively cancelled or the event fails to qualify under the Payout Criterion, or any other circumstance prevents normal resolution, then the market for "Event does not qualify" resolves to Yes and all other markets will resolve to No. If the event is postponed, and such a postponement is announced by the end of the following calendar day to be rescheduled to a time within 14 calendar days, the markets shall remain open.
