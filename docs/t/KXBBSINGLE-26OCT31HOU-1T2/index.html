Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Housewife is Tate McRae's new solo single, out Oct. 15, 2026, so its first full tracking week feeds the Oct. 31 chart. Her solo singles have peaked at No. 3 at best, and her only No. 1 was a duet with Morgan Wallen. Taking the top two would mean a career-best solo debut against unknown competition, so YES is unlikely but possible.

Wrong if: Very strong first-week streams for Housewife (for example, a big US Spotify debut near the top with sustained daily volume) during a week with no blockbuster album or chart-topper, leading to a No. 1 or No. 2 Hot 100 debut.

After seeing the price

The 40% price likely reflects first-week streaming data I couldn't access, so I raised my estimate from 6% to 20%. A top-two solo debut would still beat McRae's best solo peak of No. 3 against unknown competition, so I lean NO.
