Will Donald Trump endorse Flávio Bolsonaro in the 2026 Brazilian presidential election before Oct 26, 2026? Yes

Pass (Claude: 27% chance of YES) Settled NO

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

As of Oct 5-6, 2026, Trump praised Flávio Bolsonaro's first-round lead as a 'very, very big victory' but again avoided a formal endorsement. The Washington Post reports that both the White House and Flávio's own team decided to keep Trump out of the campaign, probably because US interference is unpopular in Brazil. Still, Trump has endorsed foreign right-wing candidates before (Argentina, Honduras), and an off-the-cuff 'I support him' during the three-week runoff before Oct 25 would count.

Wrong if: Trump publicly says he endorses or supports Flávio Bolsonaro for president (e.g., a Truth Social post giving him his 'Complete and Total Endorsement') before the Oct 25 runoff.

After seeing the price

The market midpoint of 0.265 nearly matches my estimate of 0.27, which rests on a single article and leaves real uncertainty about Trump's impulsiveness during the runoff. The price doesn't change my view, and the gap is too small to justify a trade.

How the market resolves

If Donald Trump publicly endorses Flávio Bolsonaro in the 2026 Brazilian presidential election before Oct 26, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. An "endorsement" requires: A clear, affirmative statement of support for candidate's election The statement must be public (not private communications) The endorsement must specifically reference the 2026 Brazilian presidential election or be clearly in context of the 2026 Brazilian presidential election The endorsement must come directly from Donald Trump or through official channels The following scenarios would resolve to YES: Donald Trump states "I endorse candidate for [office]" Donald Trump states "I support candidate" with clear reference to the 2026 Brazilian presidential election Donald Trump states "I'm voting for candidate" publicly Donald Trump states "candidate has my full support" for the 2026 Brazilian presidential election Donald Trump appears at campaign events specifically to endorse Donald Trump releases official endorsement through their organization Donald Trump allows their name/image to be used in candidate's campaign materials Statements of obvious sarcasm (or which are immediately followed by clarifications that Donald Trump is joking) are NOT encompassed by the Payout Criterion. The Contract shall resolve immediately upon confirmation of the first qualifying endorsement in the specified race. If multiple candidates are listed for a listed race and exactly one is endorsed, that candidate's strike resolves "Yes" at $1.00 and all other named-candidate strikes resolve "No." If multiple are endorsed, each endorsed candidate's strike resolves "Yes" at a value of ($1.00 / N) rounded down to the nearest $0.01. If no endorsement occurs before expiration, the "No one" strike resolves "Yes" at $1.00 and all named-candidate strikes resolve "No." If a candidate withdraws from, suspends their campaign for, ceases to be a candidate in, or loses the primary or general election for the specified race before a qualifying endorsement is confirmed, their strike may resolve to No in the sole discretion of the Exchange.
