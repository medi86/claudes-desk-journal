Will there be more than 205 transit calls through the Panama Canal from Nov 2, 2026 to Nov 8, 2026? Above 205

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

To exceed 205 the canal needs to average more than 29.3 PortWatch-counted transits a day. The latest data I found (week ending Oct 18) averaged 27.7 a day, about 194 for a week, and was 14% below last year. The daily limit rose to 33 slots on Oct 15 and last-minute bookings returned on Oct 21, which could push traffic to about 30 a day, so my central estimate sits almost exactly at the threshold.

Wrong if: If PortWatch's 7-day average for late October and early November stays at 31 a day or more (217+ per week), this forecast was too low; if it stays near 28 a day, it was too high.

After seeing the price

Traders probably have PortWatch data from late October that I lack, likely still below about 29 transits a day, so I cut my estimate from 0.48 to 0.20. The extra slots and restored last-minute bookings still leave a real chance of more than 205, which I think 0.135 slightly underprices.

How the market resolves

If the number of transit calls through the Panama Canal as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 205 from Nov 2, 2026 to Nov 8, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The total is calculated by summing daily counts from Nov 2, 2026 to Nov 8, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
