Will DNC Services Corp / Democratic National Committee report more than $26M in cash on hand at the end of Q3? Above $26M

Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 81¢ (from 7% YES) Resting Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

FEC filings show the DNC had $16.9M cash on hand at Aug. 31, 2026, against $17.6M in debt, and its cash has stayed between about $14M and $17M all year. Strong summer fundraising (over $14M in July-August) barely moved the balance because spending kept pace. Topping $26M would take a net gain of more than $9M in September, the heaviest spending month before a midterm, which almost never happens without a one-off windfall or new borrowing.

Wrong if: The DNC's September monthly FEC report (filed around Oct. 20) shows cash on hand above $26M, for example because of a very large transfer from joint fundraising committees, a major donor surge, or new loan money still in the account on Sept. 30.

After seeing the price

The 21% midpoint likely reflects a thin market or speculation rather than any reported September windfall. Cash stood at $16.9M on Aug. 31 and has ranged $14-17M all year, so topping $26M at Sept. 30 would take an unprecedented one-month gain. I raised my estimate slightly to 7% for news I couldn't check, but that is still far below the market.
