Will Donald Trump attend UFC 334? Yes

Pass (Claude: 25% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

UFC 334 is the annual Madison Square Garden card on Nov 14, 2026. Trump attended that card in 2023 and 2024 but skipped it in 2025, and as president he has gone to only a few UFC events a year. With the event coming just after the midterms and no reported plans for him to attend, a YES is plausible but less likely than not.

Wrong if: Dana White or the White House announcing in the week before Nov 14 that Trump will be cageside at MSG, or Trump being photographed at UFC 334.

After seeing the price

The market's 0.265 midpoint is nearly the same as my independent 0.25 estimate, so the price doesn't change my view. The gap is smaller than the spread, so I'm passing.

How the market resolves

If Donald Trump attends UFC 334, then the market resolves to Yes.
