Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Reports from the morning of October 28 confirm the Chillicothe midterm rally was scheduled for that evening, so the event very likely took place. Trump's long rally speeches almost always cover immigration, and in his second term he routinely talks about deporting criminals, so at least one qualifying word ('deport', 'deported', 'deportation(s)') is more likely than not. Lowering the odds: the speech may focus on the local economy and candidates, he may say 'removed' or 'deporting' instead (neither counts), and there is a small chance of cancellation.

Wrong if: A full transcript or video of the Chillicothe rally showing Trump never said 'deport', 'deported', or 'deportation(s)', for example if he covered immigration only with words like 'removed' or 'deporting', or if the rally was cancelled.

After seeing the price

The market at 0.29 likely reflects better data on how often Trump has used these exact words in recent rallies, so I moved most of the way from 0.72 toward it. The remaining edge is small and rests on my weaker prior, so I'm passing.
