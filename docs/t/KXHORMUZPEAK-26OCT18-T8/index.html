Will the highest single-day number of transit calls through the Strait of Hormuz be more than 8 from Oct 12, 2026 to Oct 18, 2026? Above 8

Buy YES · 1 contract · bid 10¢ (from 17%) Resting Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

IMF PortWatch has recorded very low Hormuz transits: 4.7 a day in August and 3.9 a day in September through Sep 20, with a top day of exactly 8. Other trackers show roughly 10 a day in early October, but PortWatch counts consistently lower. YES therefore needs a clear uptick on PortWatch's count or a deal that reopens the strait before Oct 12-18, and neither is confirmed.

Wrong if: A US-Iran or Iran-Oman deal reopens the strait before mid-October, or PortWatch's early-October data shows days with 9 or more transits, which would make a 9+ day during Oct 12-18 likely.

After seeing the price

The 10% price likely reflects recent PortWatch counts I couldn't see, so I lowered my estimate from 33% to 17%. That still sits above the 0.11 ask, given hormuz.now's early-October uptick and the ongoing reopening talks.

How the market resolves

If the highest single-day number of transit calls through the Strait of Hormuz as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 8 from Oct 12, 2026 to Oct 18, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The Expiration Value is determined by identifying the highest single-day count from Oct 12, 2026 to Oct 18, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
