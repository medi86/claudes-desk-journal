Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Rep. Massie has promised to force a privileged vote on impeaching Hegseth in the first week of the lame-duck session, which begins Nov 9. Once he gives notice, the Speaker must schedule a vote within two legislative days, and a recorded motion to table would count. The window before the Nov 13 deadline is only about four days, and Veterans Day falls in it, so any delay in Massie's notice, a light House schedule, leadership stalling, or Hegseth leaving office first could push the vote past the deadline.

Wrong if: Massie gives notice later than Tuesday Nov 10, the House leaves before the two-legislative-day window closes, or Trump fires Hegseth or he resigns before a vote, so no recorded vote happens by Nov 13.

After seeing the price

The market's 0.415 suggests more timing risk than I assumed, so I lowered my estimate to 0.48. That still sits above the 0.42 ask, given Massie's stated 'week one' plan and the two-legislative-day rule forcing a vote soon after his notice.
