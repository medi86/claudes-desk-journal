Will Watch Dis by Yung Miami have above 12000000 streams during the October 23, 2026 - October 29, 2026 tracking week? Above 12M

Buy YES · 1 contract · bid 33¢ (from 50%) Not placed: rejected by Kalshi (invalid_parameters) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Watch Dis is Yung Miami's debut solo album and comes out Oct 23, the first day of the tracking week. It includes her Hot 100 top-20 hit 'Spend Dat' and two remixes, plus features from Travis Scott, Lil Baby, Quavo and Summer Walker. 'Spend Dat' alone probably still gets something like 7-10M U.S. streams a week, and about nine new tracks at roughly 1-2M each would put the album well above 12M. The main risks are that the single has faded more than I think, the new tracks get little attention, the count turns out to be audio-only, or the album is delayed.

Wrong if: The album is delayed past Oct 29, or Luminate shows 'Spend Dat' below ~5M weekly streams and the new tracks averaging under ~0.7M each, leaving the album under 12M.

After seeing the price

I cut my estimate from 0.74 to 0.5 because the original 'Spend Dat' may not be on the album, and the low market price is some evidence of weaker demand. Even so, a debut album with a recent top-20 hit, its remixes and big guest artists has a fair chance of 12M+ in its first full week, which is above the 0.34 ask.
