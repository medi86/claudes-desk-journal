Wicker Rotten Tomatoes score? Above 94

Pass (Claude: 23% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Wicker is at 94% (46/49) on Rotten Tomatoes, one point below what's needed, and has risen steadily from 91% as recent reviews came in fresh. To get above 94 it must hold about 95%+ positivity across roughly 50-80 more reviews expected by Nov 2. That's possible given the trend, but a Metacritic score of about 64 points to lukewarm critics still to come, and scores usually slip after wide release.

Wrong if: The Tomatometer showing 95% or higher on Nov 2 at 10 AM ET (e.g., the fresh streak continuing through the Oct 23 and Oct 30 releases with at most 2-3 new rotten reviews).

After seeing the price

My data is a few days old and traders likely see newer reviews since release, which probably explains the lower price, so I moved from 0.34 to 0.23. The small remaining edge isn't worth betting on with stale information.
