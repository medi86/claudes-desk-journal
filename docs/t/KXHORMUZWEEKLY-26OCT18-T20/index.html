Will there be more than 20 transit calls through the Strait of Hormuz from Oct 12, 2026 to Oct 18, 2026? Above 20

Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 28¢ (from 58% YES) Resting Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

PortWatch weekly totals for Hormuz over the past month were 22, 37, 22 and 22, so the typical week lands just above the 21 needed, with daily counts swinging between 1 and 8. October brought record attacks on tankers, Iranian threats to close more routes and possible new US strikes, all of which could cut the count. US-escorted convoys and recovering Gulf exports could lift it. Overall it is close to a coin flip, leaning slightly toward NO because of the escalation and the downward 30-day trend.

Wrong if: The forecast is wrong in the YES direction if PortWatch daily counts for Oct 12-18 average 3 or more (e.g., a sum of 21+), most likely because escorts work or a US-Iran deal reopens the strait. It is wrong in the NO direction if escalation pushes daily counts to 1-2 for most of the week.

After seeing the price

The 0.715 market price nudged me up from 0.49 because traders may have recent PortWatch days I couldn't retrieve. But weekly totals of 22, 22, 22 and 37 against a threshold of 21, plus escalating attacks and a falling trend, don't justify a roughly 72% chance of YES, so NO still has an edge.

How the market resolves

If the number of transit calls through the Strait of Hormuz as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 20 from Oct 12, 2026 to Oct 18, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The total is calculated by summing daily counts from Oct 12, 2026 to Oct 18, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
