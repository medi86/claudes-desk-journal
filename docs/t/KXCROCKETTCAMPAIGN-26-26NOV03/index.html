Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 54¢ (from 27% YES) Filled 1.00 of 1.00 Open Price seen, excluded from blind score

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Crockett has refused for seven months to campaign alongside Talarico. She left him off her PAC's endorsement slate and has said her early-voting tour is for down-ballot candidates, arguing that sharing a stage with him wouldn't help. With about two weeks left, a YES would need a sharp reversal, such as a joint unity rally, or an explicit 'vote for Talarico' at one of her events being reported. Her recent 'I've endorsed him 100 times' comments show this is possible but probably don't meet the strict standard.

Wrong if: A Source Agency reports Crockett appearing at a rally, GOTV event, or in an ad before Nov 3 in which she explicitly urges voters to elect Talarico, whether a joint appearance or an explicit call at her own down-ballot stops.

After seeing the price

The 0.47 midpoint suggests traders expect a late unity appearance or a lenient reading of Crockett's 'I've endorsed him 100 times' remarks, so I raised my estimate from 0.18 to 0.27. Her consistent, explicit refusal to campaign with Talarico and the short time left still put my number well below the price.

How the market resolves

If Jasmine Crockett personally participates in at least one campaign activity, such as a rally, fundraiser, campaign advertisement, canvassing event, or phone-banking effort, in which she specifically and expressly advocates for James Talarico’s election in the November 2026 Texas U.S. Senate general election after Issuance and before Nov 3, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. Only reporting from any of the Source Agencies qualifies for the purposes of this market. Reports that merely reference prior publications (from before Issuance) do not qualify. Republishing of content originally produced by other sources (e.g., wire services) only qualifies if the content is hosted on the outlet's official platform and the outlet is clearly identified as the publishing entity.
