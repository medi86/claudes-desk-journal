Will Google release Gemini 4 before Oct 16, 2026?

Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 49¢ (from 45% YES) Resting Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Google announced Gemini 4 Argon on Sept 30, 2026, but only for a closed group of cyber defenders (Fairwind Program), which the rules exclude. Google says Ultra subscribers and paid API customers come 'soon', and has posted pricing and added Argon to its Cloud backend. Still, the release is gated by safety work and the US government's pre-release review, and there are only about 8 days left before October 16.

Wrong if: Google making Gemini 4 Argon available to Google AI Ultra subscribers or paid Gemini API/Vertex customers before Oct 16, 2026 (would make this forecast too low); or no expansion beyond Fairwind by Oct 16 (confirms lean NO).

After seeing the price

The midpoint of 0.505 pulled me up from 0.40 to 0.45, since the market may reflect leaks or Cloud backend signals I couldn't verify. Google has no confirmed public date and is gating the release on safety and government review, with about 8 days left, so I still lean slightly NO.

How the market resolves

If Google releases Gemini 4 before Oct 16, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. Release must be to the public, outside of a closed beta, though limiting it to a high-cost subscription tier is acceptable.
