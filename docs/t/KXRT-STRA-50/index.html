Street Fighter Rotten Tomatoes score? Above 50

Pass (Claude: 57% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Recent video-game adaptations cluster around 40-60% on Rotten Tomatoes. Earlier Street Fighter films scored very poorly, but this one has a comedy-minded director, trailers that were well received for leaning into the game's campy energy, and enthusiastic cast comments. With no critic reviews found before forecasting, it's roughly a coin flip, leaning slightly below the 50% line.

Wrong if: Reviews published before or on release weekend that put the Tomatometer clearly above 60% (or clearly below 40%) by Oct 19, 2026.

After seeing the price

The market at 64.5% sits well above my 48% estimate, and traders may have early critic reactions I couldn't access, so I moved up to about 57%. The remaining gap is small relative to that information disadvantage, so I won't bet against the price.
