Will Donald Trump make above 240 Truth Social posts the week of Oct 18, 2026? >240

Pass (Claude: 19% chance of YES) Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Trump's recent typical output is about 20-25 Truth Social posts per day (roughly 140-176 a week; TrumpAlert mean 25.2/day from late June to mid-October 2026), and even his record month (861 posts in May) averaged only about 195 a week. Going above 240 requires about 35 a day, which usually takes a 150+ post binge on top of a normal week. Such binges happen occasionally, and the late-campaign midterm period may raise activity, so YES is possible but unlikely.

Wrong if: Roll Call's count for Oct 18-24, 2026 exceeds 240, for example because of one or more 100+ post late-night sprees during the week.

After seeing the price

The market at 22.5% sits above my estimate, but the week is already under way and traders likely see partial counts, and Roll Call probably counts more posts than TrumpAlert. I moved my estimate toward the market, and the remaining gap is too small to act on.

How the market resolves

If the number of Truth Social posts by Donald Trump from Oct 18, 2026 to Oct 24, 2026 is above 240, then the market resolves to Yes. How this is resolved The observation period begins at 12:00:00 AM ET on Oct 18, 2026 and ends at 11:59:59 PM ET on Oct 24, 2026. The primary resolution source for this market is Roll Call. In the event that Roll Call data is unavailable or inaccurate, alternative sources may be used at Kalshi's discretion. Payout criteria Truths, ReTruths, and Quote Truths are included in the total count. Replies are included if captured by Roll Call. "Between" is inclusive of the lower and upper bound. Doesn’t count for payout: Posts deleted as of 12:00 AM ET.
