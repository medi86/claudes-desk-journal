Will Minnesota US Senate have the smallest margin of victory in the November 3, 2026 general elections for Minnesota US Senate and Mississippi US Senate?

Pass (Claude: 57% chance of YES) Open Price seen, excluded from blind score

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Minnesota polling averages show Flanagan up about 4.5 points over Tafoya, while sparse and mostly Democratic-leaning Mississippi polls show Hyde-Smith up about 7, which favors Minnesota having the smaller margin. However, Mississippi Democrats usually hit a vote-share ceiling, and a Democratic-friendly national environment could widen Flanagan's lead past 6-8 points, so the two margins overlap heavily. Net, Minnesota is a modest favorite to be closer.

Wrong if: Flanagan wins Minnesota by more than Hyde-Smith wins Mississippi, e.g., Flanagan +9 while Colom holds Hyde-Smith to about +5.

After seeing the price

The market's 0.595 midpoint is only a few points above my estimate, and with thin Mississippi polling and heavy overlap in the likely margins, that gap is too small to be a reliable edge. I'm nudging slightly toward the market and passing.

How the market resolves

If Minnesota US Senate has the smallest margin of victory among all elections in the November 3, 2026 general elections for Minnesota US Senate and Mississippi US Senate as of Nov 10, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The margin of victory is calculated as the absolute difference in vote share (expressed as percentage) between first and second place finishers, using total votes cast as the denominator. If official results aren't available by the deadline, preliminary results from Source Agencies with at least 95% of expected vote are used. If multiple elections have identical margins and relevant options are listed, each tied option resolves to Yes at $1.00 divided by the number of tied options.
