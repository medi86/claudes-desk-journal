Godzilla Minus Zero Rotten Tomatoes score? Above 94

Buy NO · 1 contract · bid 30¢ (from 58% YES) Filled 1.00 of 1.00 Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

The film sits at 96% from 46 reviews and has held at 95-96% since its festival premiere, but YES needs it to still show 95% or higher after roughly 150-200 reviews. Reviews that arrive around wide release often pull scores down 1-3 points, and a few mixed or negative notices (Empire 3/5, Dread Central) are already in. Minus One kept a 97-99% score, so holding 95% is plausible, which makes this close to a coin flip.

Wrong if: Six or more extra rotten reviews around the Nov 6 US release, pushing the Tomatometer down to 94% or lower by Nov 9.

After seeing the price

The market's 69.5% suggests more confidence than my estimate, possibly from newer score data, so I move up partway to 58%. YES needs at least a 95.5% fresh rate across a large final review count, which leaves little room for the usual post-release drift, so NO still looks underpriced.
