Will the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee report more than $60M in cash on hand at the end of Q3? Above $60M

Buy YES · 1 contract · bid 18¢ (from 30%) Resting Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

The DCCC had $76M cash on hand at the end of August 2026, after falling about $4M in August while raising $12M. To land at or below $60M it would need to lose more than $16M net in September. That would take spending above about $30M, which is possible as fall ad spending ramps up but would be a big jump from August. End-of-quarter fundraising should partly offset the spending, so finishing above $60M is somewhat more likely than not.

Wrong if: The DCCC's September monthly FEC report (due around Oct 20, 2026) showing cash on hand at or below $60M, most likely because of heavy September independent-expenditure ad spending.

After seeing the price

The 16% price suggests traders expect very heavy September spending, so I've moved down a lot from 64%. Going from $76M to at or below $60M still takes more than $16M of net burn in one month, which I think the market is overpricing.

How the market resolves

If the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee's cash on hand is above $60 million as of September 30, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. This market resolves based on the official filing data released by the Federal Election Commission (FEC). Only the first official or non-preliminary release of the relevant statistic will be used to resolve the market, and subsequent revisions, corrections, or updates will not affect resolution.
