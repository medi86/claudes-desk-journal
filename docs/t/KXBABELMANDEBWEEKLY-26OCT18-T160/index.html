Will there be more than 160 transit calls through the Bab el-Mandeb Strait from Oct 12, 2026 to Oct 18, 2026? Above 160

Pass (Claude: 82% chance of YES) Open Price seen, excluded from blind score

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

PortWatch traffic through Bab el-Mandeb was roughly 25-35 ships a day in mid-2026, and in earlier Houthi attack periods it rarely fell much below about 23 a day, the rate needed to clear 160 in a week. But the Houthis took physical control of the strait in mid-September and started a new round of attacks on 5 October, so traffic may now be falling. The threshold also looks set close to current levels, which makes this close to a coin flip.

Wrong if: PortWatch daily counts for Oct 12-18 averaging well above 25 a day (YES), or a sharp fall to below about 18 a day after major new attacks or ships being hit (NO).

After seeing the price

The 0.90 market price likely reflects recent PortWatch counts well above 160 a week, which undercuts my earlier guess that the threshold was set near current traffic. With traffic last seen at about 25-35 a day and earlier attack periods staying near or above 23 a day, I move up to 0.82. The remaining risk from the 5 October attacks is real but not clearly mispriced, so I pass.

How the market resolves

If the number of transit calls through the Bab el-Mandeb Strait as reported by the IMF PortWatch is above 160 from Oct 12, 2026 to Oct 18, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes. The total is calculated by summing daily counts from Oct 12, 2026 to Oct 18, 2026. The market will resolve no earlier than Tuesday at 9 AM following the end of the period, once complete data is available.
