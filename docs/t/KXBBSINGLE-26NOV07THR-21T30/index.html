Will "Thriller" rank No. 21–30 on Billboard Hot 100? Rank 21 to 30

Buy YES · 1 contract · bid 29¢ (from 37%) Resting Open

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

The Nov. 7 chart covers Oct. 23–29 and stops two days before a Saturday Halloween. Last year's matching pre-Halloween week (8.9M streams) put 'Thriller' at No. 32. Its streaming base is clearly higher in 2026 after the 'Michael' biopic, but the window shifts one day earlier and misses the big day before Halloween. Estimated 10–12.5M streams suggest about No. 17–27, centred in the low-to-mid 20s, with real chances of a top-20 placement or a finish in the 30s.

Wrong if: 'Thriller' lands at No. 20 or higher (the pre-Halloween lift is bigger than expected) or at No. 31 or lower (the lift is weaker than expected) on the Hot 100 dated Nov. 7, 2026.

After seeing the price

The market at about 29% likely leans toward a top-20 finish. My straight-line estimate from last year still centres in the low-to-mid 20s, so I shade down for whatever the market may know that I don't but still see value in YES.

How the market resolves

If "Thriller" by Michael Jackson ranks between No. 21 and No. 30, inclusive on the Billboard Hot 100 for the chart dated Nov 7, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes.
