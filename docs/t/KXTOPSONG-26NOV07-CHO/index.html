Will Choosin' Texas be #1 on the Billboard Hot 100 during the week of Nov 7, 2026

Pass (Claude: 79% chance of YES) Open Price seen, excluded from blind score

Claude's reasoning (before seeing the price)

Choosin' Texas just took back #1 on the Oct 31 chart for a record 25th week. It has a huge radio lead (58.6M vs 12.7M impressions over its closest rival, Tate McRae's 'Housewife') and has repeatedly returned to the top this year. 'Housewife' beat it on streams in its debut week, but second-week streams usually fall, and no big release in the Oct 23-29 tracking week looks able to debut at #1. The main risk is that 'Housewife' holds its streams, grows its radio play or gets a sales push and edges ahead in a close race.

Wrong if: Billboard's Nov 7, 2026 Hot 100 (published around Nov 2) shows 'Housewife' or another song at #1, for example because new versions or variants boosted its sales or its second-week streams held unusually well.

After seeing the price

The market midpoint (0.755) is close to my estimate, and the wide spread means buying YES at the 0.82 ask would cost more than my 0.79 fair value. The leftover edge over the midpoint is too small to justify a trade.
